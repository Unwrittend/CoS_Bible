### Gaille

  - **Player: Dorothy** (Discord: [SakuraScout](../players.md))
  - **Aesthetic archetype:** Gothic necromancer — controlled, studied, aesthetically committed to her practice
  - **Race/Class:** Female Elf, Necromancer
  - **Physical description:** Black hair, hazel eyes, pale skin, dark robes, hooded cloak, water opal pendant
  - **Personality:** Inquisitive
  - **Key relationships:** [Void Child](../npcs/khirad.md)
  - **Personal Item:** Amber Necklace (Void Child)
  - **Key Plot Points:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  0.5 - Met Void Child
2.  6.0 - Receive amber that she licked due to Void Child peer pressure. Void child more sentient afterwards
3.  8.0 - Learned that the resinites were related to Amber
4.  9.0 - Shared her own resinite/Amber-prison lore with the rest of the party at the Tser Falls campfire, in verse — the table's reactions to it were mixed (lie/half-truth/truth all called by different NPCs), so it's unclear above-table whether the party actually absorbed it as reliable information or as "just a story."
5.  10.0 - Picked up loose pages of [Stella Watcher](../npcs/stella-watcher.md)'s diary with Mage Hand. Met [Kasimir](../npcs/kasimir-velikov.md) at the Vistani camp; told him she's researching the Amber Temple and showed him her amber charm. **When Kasimir touched it, the amber flickered and turned noticeably more yellow — the first time it has ever reacted.** He asked to speak with her privately later; she deferred until after the [Arabelle](../npcs/arabelle.md) situation.
