### Theren

  - **Player: Tina**
  - **Aesthetic archetype:** Stately yet roguish — the contradiction is the character
  - **Race/Class:** Elf, Life Cleric
  - **Personality notes:** Very gentlemanly. Flirtatious. Loyal to a fault.
  - **Key relationships:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  [Reysatra](../npcs/reysatra.md) - his sister
2.  Titania - Made a fey contract with

<!-- end list -->

  - **Personal Item:** Hallow Dragon's Tooth
  - **Key Plot Points:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  Between Sessions 9 and 10 - Stormed off after being told his sister could be trapped forever and spent the night with [Eliza](../npcs/eliza.md). While he was gone, the rest of the party stole his gold (GM-confirmed; Theren doesn't know who did it and suspects [Reyna](reyna.md)).
2.  10.0 - Discovered his coin purse was empty. Stopped [Luvash](../npcs/luvash.md) whipping [Alexei](../npcs/alexei.md) at the Vistani camp, earning inspiration for the roleplay.
3.  11.0 - Insisted the party guard [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) when she chose to stay with the refugees. Identified the Tser Falls sedative herb in [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)'s pie (Medicine). Cured the child [Gen](../npcs/gen.md) with *lesser restoration* and declared her his child. Autopsied two refugee corpses and found icy boils incubating gel-encased worm pupae (Nature, helped by [Lyasra](lyasra.md)).
