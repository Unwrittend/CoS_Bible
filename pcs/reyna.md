  - **Key Plot Points:**

<!-- end list -->

1.  8.0 - Joined party
2.  8.0 - Looking for Dismas but narrowly missed him at the Vistani Encampent at Tser Falls.
3.  9.0 - Told the region's classic "first vampire" origin myth (the baron, his brother, the crossbow) as her own story at the campfire; judged half-truth by most listeners. Separately mentioned she has personally killed one vampire before (beheaded and left it for the sun) — not the first vampire, just "kind of a bitch."
4.  10.0 - Bought out [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)'s last seven pies for 7 gp (she declined to eat one), traded candy to [Elaine](../npcs/elaine-groze.md) for [Stella Watcher](../npcs/stella-watcher.md)'s diary, and read it. Her background feature (commoners who look into her eyes fear her) frightened Elaine. Split off to the Barovian refugee camp and gathered rumors while the others were at the Vistani camp.
5.  11.0 - Touched an infected refugee (she is immune to disease); Divine Sense found no undead. Cured [Emeric](../npcs/emeric.md) and [Magda](../npcs/magda.md)'s worms with Lay on Hands. Adopted the refugee child [Gen](../npcs/gen.md) into a baby-carrier sling with candy. Got the party through Vallaki's gate at night: her background feature and Persuasion failed, then Intimidation plus a 10 gp bribe worked. Plans to gather information overnight in commoner clothes.
6.  11.5 - In Vallaki, sent [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) to distract the guards and went with [Lyasra](lyasra.md) and Gen to the Blue Water Inn. Knocked out a guard of the watch who wanted to take them in for bribery and breaking in, took a silver longknife off him, and broke down the inn's door. **Lost an eye** to a crossbow-wielding clown inside and went down; Lyasra healed her. Her Divine Sense found St. Andral's Church hallowed (a warmth she'd not felt in Barovia) but felt it flicker out for a moment. Tried to handcuff "Ireena" (really [Cassandra](cassandra-priam.md)) to a pew, which Cassandra refused, and slept on a front pew waiting to speak with [Father Lucien](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md).
