# SECTION 6: REUSABLE PROMPT TEMPLATES

Copy, fill in the blanks, and paste these into any session to get consistent outputs.

## NPC Dialogue & Scene — Screenplay Format

Write a scene for \[SCENE/LOCATION\] involving \[NPC NAME(S)\] in the following situation:

  

\[DESCRIBE THE SCENE, WHAT PRECEDES IT, AND WHAT THE PLAYERS HAVE SAID OR DONE\].

  

Format as a screenplay:

  

  - **Scene heading** (INT./EXT. — LOCATION — TIME), used at the start and at any hard location or time change
  - **Action/stage direction** in short, present-tense lines — sparse and functional, not descriptive prose
  - **Character cues in ALL CAPS**, followed by dialogue
  - **Parentheticals** for tone, physical business, or delivery, used sparingly — only where they change how a line should be read or performed
  - **Conditional branches** marked clearly, e.g. *(IF the players ask about X:)* or *(Unless the players intervene:)*, so branching dialogue stays legible in a single document
  - **CONT'D** for a character continuing after a stage direction interrupts them mid-speech

  

Draw on each NPC's established characterization: \[PASTE KEY TRAITS FROM THEIR PROFILE ABOVE\].

  

Voice reference: \[e.g., Dragnacarta's Gertruda / Mad Mary broken-grief register\].

  

Keep exposition (backstory, motivations, secret agendas) **out of the dialogue** unless the character would plausibly say it aloud to the players at this point in the relationship — flag anything GM-only as a separate note beneath the scene, not spoken content.

  

## Character Letter / Journal Entry

Write a \[letter / journal entry\] from \[CHARACTER NAME\] to \[RECIPIENT / themselves\].

  

Situation: \[What has just happened that motivates this writing? What emotional state are they in?\]

  

Voice: \[Reference their profile above — key traits, speech patterns, internal register\].

  

This should read as a real document this character would produce, not a plot summary.

  

Length: \[SHORT — half a page / MEDIUM — one page / LONG — two pages equivalent\].

  

Follow with design notes.

  

## Encounter Atmosphere — Mid-Session

The players are in the middle of \[ENCOUNTER / SCENE\]. I need a 2–3 sentence atmospheric

injection to read aloud when \[SPECIFIC TRIGGER — e.g., they enter the room, the NPC

reveals something, the mood shifts\].

  

Gothic register. Concise. Punchy. No mechanical information — atmosphere only.
