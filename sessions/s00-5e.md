### Session 0.5e - A Call Home

**Players:** [Theren](../pcs/theren.md) **Channel:** \#0-5e-a-call-home

  

**Summary:**

  

  - Two days into a diplomatic assignment, Theren's ironwood pendant burns against his chest — his mother [Violet](../npcs/violet-larkwing.md) sends a telepathic message: *"Amarthan-elen, return. Melimoialë is unwell. Hurry."* He rides through the night with his cousin [Amogano](../npcs/amogano.md), cutting a two-day journey to one
  - Arriving at the village, the guardsman (his father's old colleague, described as an uncle-figure) strips the reins from their hands and directs them immediately to the Healer's Hall — a yurt embedded in the base of an old oak tree
  - Amogano is stopped at the entrance by guards; Theren alone is permitted inside
  - [Ninthalor](../npcs/ninthalor.md) and Theren's grandfather await him with grim faces. The scene: Violet weeping over [Reysatra](../npcs/reysatra.md)'s unconscious body, the eldest cleric of the Elven Church present with hands crossed in prayer, archaic runes sealing the room
  - The eldest cleric produces a **soul scrying wand** — it does not react over Reysatra's body. The verdict: *"Her soul has been lost."*
  - Theren shoves to her side, takes her hand, and prays to **Rillifane Rallathil** — a desperate, jumbled prayer of denial. He rounds on Ninthalor in anger
  - Ninthalor responds with cold pragmatism: soul loss is not unheard of in Arondir Lethyr, especially among those who don't make Fey contracts. Violet furiously defends Reysatra and insists foul magic is at play
  - On a Religion check (dirty 20), Rillifane Rallathil responds — a whisper of wind at Theren's ear: *"Drawn upon my power, she does. Far away, and unalive, Uluchnir Lav-Varno Eryn is. Stubborn. Trapped."*
  - **Scene closes open-ended** — the god has confirmed Reysatra's soul is alive but trapped elsewhere. The [Hollow Dragon's Tooth](../items.md#theren--hollow-dragons-tooth) item thread is now live. The intention agreed on by Theren's player and the DMs is that Theren makes a deal with Titania

  

**Key revelations & consequences:**

  

  - Reysatra's soul is confirmed **lost from her body but not destroyed** — Rillifane Rallathil's message implies she is trapped somewhere, drawing on his power, alive in some form
  - The name *Uluchnir Lav-Varno Eryn* appears to be how Rillifane refers to Reysatra — worth tracking as her divine/true name
  - Ninthalor is established as cold and politically pragmatic; his relationship with Theren is adversarial and carries long history
  - Violet Larkwing (Theren's mother, a druid) is fiercely protective and suspects supernatural foul play — she may be a useful ally or source of information
  - Amogano (Theren's cousin) is established as a traveling companion and trusted figure, though kept outside the inner circle for this scene
  - The **Hollow Dragon's Tooth** as a soul vessel is now narratively motivated — Theren has reason to be actively searching for a container for his sister's soul

  

**Key NPCs established:**

  

  - **Violet Larkwing** — Theren's mother, druid, emotionally volatile in grief, suspects foul magic
  - **Ninthalor** — Elder figure, stone-faced pragmatist, adversarial toward Theren; likely Elven Church-aligned
  - **Amogano** — Theren's cousin, loyal traveling companion
  - **The Eldest Cleric** — Unnamed; possesses the soul scrying wand; significant religious authority in the village

  

**Open threads:**

  

  - Where is Reysatra's soul trapped, and what drew it out of her body?
  - What does *Uluchnir Lav-Varno Eryn* mean — is this her true name, a title, or a location reference?
  - What are Violet's and Eltharion Eryndor's stated wishes for Reysatra's fate that the eldest cleric referenced?
  - Ninthalor's mention of Fey contracts as protective — does this connect to Theren's own contract with Titania?
