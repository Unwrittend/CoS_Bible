### Session 3.5b - The Shadow Theatre

**Players:** [Cassandra](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md)  
**Channel:** \#3-5b-the-shadow-theatre

  

**Summary:**

  

  - Cassandra falls asleep at the Blood of the Vine tavern, wine in hand, and enters a dream sequence set in a grand shadow puppet theatre
  - She finds herself seated in a private balcony beside **Ismerk** — a slightly off version of Ismark — who carries a raven on his shoulder that Cassandra correctly identifies as not belonging to him
  - Cassandra rolls low on Wisdom and clocks the raven as wrong but cannot fully place why; on a later Perception 20 she notices the raven's eyes move independently, tracking her
  - Ismerk proposes a three-card Tarokka reading framed as being for him; Cassandra correctly deduces early that the reading is for her benefit, not his
  - Each card drawn triggers a shadow puppet play on the stage below depicting the history of the Rozana:
      
      - **Card 1 — The Missionary:** The Weaver is shown blessing her people in still water until her power fails; she looks directly at Cassandra's balcony before her spirals fray but hold
      - **Card 2 — The Berserker:** The Huntress is shown on Yester Hill, betrayed by a kneeling follower who leads a purple-shadowed figure to her shrine; she is defeated, the Gulthias Tree grows from where she falls, the betrayer transforms into a wolf
      - **Card 3 — The Broken One:** The Seeker is shown moving urgently between three sickly fanes before choosing retreat over confrontation with the purple shadow; she transfers her divine essence as a single feather into a smaller mortal woman, who then sits at a table and picks up a deck of cards that orbit her — exactly as Cassandra's do
  - Ismerk delivers the line *"She chose to become small so the truth could survive"* and asks Cassandra directly *"Who is this future for?"* — Cassandra deflects with mania and self-deprecation
  - Ismerk's voice continues though his lips stop moving; the room grows dramatically colder; Cassandra correctly deduces the entity is not [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)-aligned due to the cold being attributed to *"another's aspect"*
  - **An uninvited fourth card — The Horseman — appears on the table, drawn by no one**
  - The entity speaking through Ismerk reacts with alarm: *"You! You were not invited!"*
  - On an Arcana 16, Cassandra briefly sees Ismerk's face replaced by a wooden mannequin's and perceives a magical aura the size of a man surrounding the raven
  - The shadow play resumes uninvited: the three sisters huddle at a fire while a fourth figure stands apart, reaching toward a hand made not of shadow but of **pure darkness**; the fourth sister walks into it and her form becomes night
  - Cassandra wakes gasping

  

**Key revelations & consequences:**

  

  - Cassandra has now witnessed the complete myth of the Rozana — the Weaver, the Huntress, the Seeker, and the existence of a fourth sister who fell
  - The Seeker's avatar ([Madam Eva](../npcs/madam-eva.md)) is now confirmed through imagery — the feather transfer and the orbiting cards are a direct mirror of Cassandra herself; the Seeker chose Cassandra deliberately
  - The fourth card and the Devourer's appearance were uninvited and alarmed the Ismerk-entity, suggesting the Devourer's reach exceeds what even the dream's architect intended

  

**Key NPCs established:**

  

  - **Ismerk** — Dream avatar; wooden mannequin beneath the human face; the raven carries a magical aura the size of a man. Play on Ismark.
