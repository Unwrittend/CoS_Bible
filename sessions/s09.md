### Session 9 - Campfire Confessions & the Woodcutter's Bride

**Players:** Reyna, Lyasra, Cassandra, Theren, Gaille (party currently down to five active PCs — Dismas retired to NPC, Jacob on hiatus)

**Summary:**

- Completed the campfire storytelling scene at the Tser Falls Vistani camp, deferred from Session 8. Everyone present told a story and was judged true/half-truth/lie by the in-fiction NPCs (Irena, Arturi, Eliza, Stanimir):
    - **Reyna** told the region's well-known origin myth of the first vampire (a baron, jealous of his younger brother's marriage, has him killed; the brother returns and shoots him with a crossbow; grief and rage transform the baron into the first vampire, cursed against sunlight, running water, and uninvited entry). Judged mostly half-truth/true by the table. Separately, Reyna mentioned she has personally destroyed one vampire before (beheaded, left for the sun) — not the first one.
    - **Eliza** told the Vistani people's own founding parable: a wounded, unnamed man of royal blood was taken in, protected, and safely returned home, in exchange granting the Vistani permanent welcome and safe passage. Judged true by most listeners.
    - **Theren** shared a personal story from his early acolyte days: tending survivors of a plague-and-siege in a village called Anders, only to watch people mistake the survivors for plague-bringers and burn them alive as well. Judged true.
    - **Stanimir** told the planned Lugdana/Holy Symbol of Ravenkind poem (per Section 13.3's plan) — sung in verse, describing Lugdana's fight against a demon lord on Yester Hill and the symbol's origin. Judged a half-truth: "Lugdana surely existed... we do not know where she is."
    - **Cassandra** told an origin story for her raven familiar Lycius, initially framing him as a foundling she rescued from an abandoned nest — this was called out as the session's designated "lie." The true version, revealed above-table: Lycius is a multi-generation family familiar inherited across several Priam wizards, which is why his coloring carries no distinguishing primordial mark.
    - **Arturi** (present and named for the first time to the party) told a fable about a fox who loses his adopted kit to vengeful sparrows, then uses rats to destroy the sparrows in turn — read by Theren's player, on a high insight check, as carrying unusually strong emotional intensity; likely a coded retelling of his own history.
    - **Gaille** shared her own resinite/Amber-prison lore in verse (drawing on what she learned in Session 0.5d) — reactions from the table's NPCs were mixed (lie/half-truth/true called by different listeners).
    - Everyone who told a story received inspiration for the session.
- At the map afterward, the party reviewed their Tarokka card readings with Irena:
    - Confirmed the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (Five of Swords, the Myrmidon) is at **Luna Lake** specifically — the only one of the two previously-candidate lakes with hills, matching "a den of wolves in the hills."
    - Confirmed the Sun Sword/Amber Temple lead (Eight of Glyphs) points to a Holt "led by a dragon, died over a hundred years ago," reachable via an offshoot of the **Luna River**.
    - A new "ghost card" clue surfaced: an ally described as "a soul unmoored from its body, bound still to duty... a blade that does not know it has already fallen." The party speculated (unconfirmed, GM did not commit either way) that this could be Strahd's own slain brother rather than the previously-assumed Petrina Velikanova.
    - Confirmed only three bastions of civilization remain in the valley: the village of Barovia, Vallaki, and Krezk.
    - Party decided to head for Vallaki — to look for Gertruda among the refugees there, seek priestly protection for Irena, and follow the angel lead.
- Traveling toward Vallaki, the party crossed a great stone bridge over a waterfall and canyon. They found the corpse of a dead owlbear and several vultures behaving erratically — some flying in an unnaturally disoriented pattern, others transfixed motionless on their own reflections in the river. Cause unconfirmed.
- On the road to Vallaki, the party encountered a wealthy, dark-skinned noblewoman of confirmed noble lineage, directing a long line of undead carrying cut lumber, accompanied by at least one wizard ally. She waved the party off rather than engaging — the party (led by Reyna) chose to press the confrontation instead of walking on.
- A large combat followed. The noblewoman was revealed to be a vampire: she fought airborne over the canyon (at one point grappling and carrying Reyna aloft), used mist form, and deployed a choking/suffocation mist attack not previously seen from any vampire in this campaign. The party pursued her into mist form and finished her.
- Her death was unusual: she screamed, the sky visibly "split open," and she was described as being deleted from existence outright ("nothing remains") rather than the normal dust-and-corpse vampire ending.
- The instant she died, every undead she was animating collapsed simultaneously across a very wide area — not just her visible wood-hauling line, but others "up to and beyond the castle," including separate groups chopping wood elsewhere in the forest — confirming she was centrally coordinating a much larger, castle-scale logistics/construction operation than the immediate encounter suggested.
- The party was left with a large stockpile of cut lumber and debated burning it versus using it as tribute to gain entry into Vallaki — unresolved as the session ended.

**Key revelations & consequences:**

- The completed campfire scene substantially followed Section 13.3's plan (Eliza and Stanimir told their assigned stories), but also included unplanned stories from Arturi and Gaille, and Irena's own story slot was not used.
- Cassandra's raven familiar Lycius now has a confirmed, more mundane true origin (inherited family familiar) than the "rescued foundling" framing used previously.
- The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind's location is narrowed from "Luna or Baratok" to Luna Lake specifically; Argonvost's Holt is now tied to an offshoot of the Luna River.
- This session provides the first actual-play evidence that Strahd is running a large-scale undead-labor construction operation, coordinated by at least one noble-lineage vampire lieutenant — advancing (though not fully confirming) the previously idea-stage notion that Strahd's military/domestic footprint is expanding (see Section 10.5).
- The party's theory that the Tarokka "lost soul" ally could be Strahd's brother rather than Petrina is a party theory prompted by Reyna's own campfire story, not a GM-confirmed fact — flagged in Strahd's and Casimir's entries so it isn't mistaken for established lore.

**Key NPCs established/developed:**

- **The Woodcutter's Bride** (not named to the party) — new vampire NPC, noble lineage, commands a castle-scale undead labor operation; apparently destroyed this session in an unusual manner. See full Section 4 entry.
- **GM-only canon (confirmed 2026-09-24, unknown to the party):** she was **Ludmilla Vilisevic**, Strahd's eldest bride, and she was **not destroyed** — an emergency dimensional portal returned her to Castle Ravenloft when she hit 0 HP. The split sky and "nothing remains" were the portal.
- **Arturi** — now directly encountered and named to the party; his campfire story reads as more emotionally significant than the "flavor only" framing previously logged for him.
- **Eliza** and **Stanimir** — both confirmed as having told their planned campfire stories in play.
