### Session 0.5d - Pursuit of Knowledge

**Players:** Gaille **Channel:** \#0-5d-pursuit-of-knowledge

  

**Summary:**

  

  - Gaille arrives at the small town of Menis, having heard that a Resinite once passed through. Her goal: find information about the Resin Shrine and its secrets of eternal life
  - Two friendly guards direct her to the town library and its librarian, **Miri**
  - Miri greets her with an unsettling foreknowledge — *"A little dove told me a girl fond of secrets may be coming my way"* — and speaks entirely in rhyme. This appears to be a genuine characterization trait, not an affectation
  - Gaille asks about the Resinites and the Resin Shrine. Miri responds by performing a **doroq** — a half-chant, half-song epic — which reveals the following:
      
      - The Resin Shrine is not merely a repository of knowledge; it is a **prison** built to contain an ancient, unkillable darkness
      - The entity drew wandering souls to it with promises of eternal, deathless things
      - The Resinites could not destroy it, so they **caged it** in amber and remained to watch over it — singing what Miri calls "the watching hymn"
  - Gaille correctly identifies that the amber charm Miri shows her is a fragment of the prison structure. Detect Magic confirms **abjuration magic** emanating from it
  - As Gaille holds the charm and casts Detect Magic, she hears **childlike laughter** — Miri goes unnervingly still, and the laughter continues. When Gaille asks if something wants to speak with her, the scene ends on an open, ominous beat
  - Miri places the resin charm in Gaille's hand before the scene closes — **Gaille now possesses it**

  

**Key revelations & consequences:**

  

  - The Resin Shrine is NOT confirmed as the **Amber Temple** — Gaille now has foundational lore about what it is and why it exists, acquired before entering Barovia
  - The amber charm is a shard of the prison framework — this is the **moment of acquisition** for the Amber Shard item
  - Khirad's presence is already detectable through the charm before Gaille has accepted any of its gifts — the childlike laughter is the vestige's first contact
  - Miri's foreknowledge (*"a little dove told me"*) is unexplained — she may have a connection to the ravens of Barovia or to the Seeker's network

  

**Key NPCs established:**

  

  - **Miri** — Elderly librarian of Menis; speaks entirely in rhyme; possesses foreknowledge of Gaille's arrival; goes unnervingly still when Khirad's laughter manifests. Her true nature and affiliations are unresolved

  

**Open threads:**

  

  - What hidden information did Gaille miss in the doroq due to the failed rolls?
  - What is Miri's connection to the ravens — or to the Seeker?
  - The childlike laughter at the session's end: is Khirad already attempting to initiate contact, or simply amused by being detected?
  - Gaille now carries the Amber Shard — when does Stage One of Khirad's corruption begin?
