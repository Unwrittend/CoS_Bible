### Session 6 - Wailing Tree

- Players leave Barovia with Ireena to search for the X on Von Richten's cache map
- They meet Kreza and another Barovia Scout
- Encounter an evil tree that was once a magnolia but had the gulthias tree grafted onto it.
    - The tree is part of the forest druid's attempt to revive the forest fane
- Players slay the tree and receive part of Von Richten's flesh map. A map of caches.
    - Implied that Van Richten was researching the wailing tree. Intended to refer to Von Richten looking into the Summer Fane
