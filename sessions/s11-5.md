### Session 11.5 - Welcome to Vallaki

**Players:** [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md), [Lyasra](../pcs/lyasra.md) (Andreia), [Cassandra](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md) (Mary); [Gaille](../pcs/gaille.md) (Dorothy) and [Theren](../pcs/theren.md) (Tina) were tagged but stayed in the background. [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md), [Gen](../npcs/gen.md), [Magda](../npcs/magda.md) and [Emeric](../npcs/emeric.md) present as NPCs. **Channel:** Discord, 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-28.

**Context:** Online RP. It picks up the moment the party bribed the gate guards at the end of [Session 11](s11.md), before the fire and the lullaby heard at the gate, and runs to midnight on the same night. The next live session picks up from here.

**Summary:**

- **The gate.** The two guards counted the bribe and raised the gate. The one who took the coin, young and scrawny with patchy brown facial hair, had a hand crossbow strapped to his forearm under his cloak. He told the party to cross the ankle-deep stream between the gate and the town quickly, "else you'll be eatin' rot." As Lyasra crossed, he took out a scroll and charcoal and marked something down.
- **First look at Vallaki.** Much bigger than Barovia. The party noted a **stockyard** just off the main road (crates, hay, animal pens), a flier on the eastern barracks reading "Blue Water Inn — The best wine in town!", and far down the main road **a single steeple lit gold**. Not one window showed a candle; several houses were barred shut.
- **The split.** Reyna wanted to bribe an inn for its biggest room and "strap down the vampire bait"; Lyasra mocked her for it, and bristled at being called "cleric." Ireena had the large group hide in the stockyard while Reyna and Lyasra found lodging, and agreed to keep the guards busy as a distraction (for the biggest bed at the inn). Reyna and Lyasra set off with Gen, who asked Lyasra to be her "buddy" because she doesn't like the dark; Lyasra lit a faint light on her amulet for her.
- **The Blue Water Inn.** The flier gave no directions (the GM: "for a certain reason"). Lyasra followed her nose, through a town that smelled of garlic, to the middle of town: a signpost with a blue mug, and the smell of wine and bread. Every building was silent, the inn included.
- **The guard.** Something moved nearby (roof, alley or sewer grate; they couldn't tell). A guard unshuttered a lantern and stepped out of the alley. "All will be well, foreigner," he said, like a prayer. Curfew had rung an hour before; "the Burgomaster is generous to those who keep our laws and less to those that don't." He said he'd know if Gen were from Vallaki, and told Lyasra to put out her light. Lyasra said Gen was a rescued child they were returning to her parents in **Krezk**, and doused the light. He believed her: "You Krezkans ought to know better than to come to Vallaki on a full moon," and said they were lucky he wasn't **Captain [Izek](../npcs/izek-strazani.md)**, who'd have had them in irons first.
- Reyna tried to brush him off with an imperious speech and a clumsily palmed gold piece, then tried the inn's door: locked. The guard turned on her: bribing an officer of the watch and breaking and entering were crimes, and she'd come with him for questioning. To Lyasra he turned a sickening smile: she'd be taken **to the Manor for questioning**, and "the Baron can host [the girl] for the night. He will have all the necessary leashes for a full moon." Lyasra said she'd go anywhere, so long as she could protect the child.
- **Reyna knocked the guard out** with a punch to the head while he leaned in to hear her. As he crumpled, **the midnight bell** began to toll. On its second ring, **ravens cawed** in chorus; on the third, bursts of firelight like fireballs flared **beyond the city gate**, all from one moving source.
- Lyasra suggested the church instead. Reyna searched the guard (a **silver longknife** and her gold coin back) and **broke down the Blue Water Inn's door**.
- **The clown.** Crossbow bolts came straight through the door. A **clown** with a hand crossbow ("Ah, an audience! I'm afraid the show doesn't start until morning, and the box office is very much closed") tumbled behind an overturned table, drew a second crossbow and shot Reyna again. **A bolt through her visor took one of Reyna's eyes** (the player chooses which), and she fell. "And that, ladies and gentlemen, is what we call a showstopper!" He invited Lyasra, "the sensible half of this act," to patch up her friend: "I'd hate to bill the house for a funeral."
- Lyasra complimented him ("she did it to herself"), set Gen and her own weapons down, took Reyna's weapons, healed her, wished him a good evening and walked off towards the church with Gen asleep in her arms. Reyna, conscious again and one eye short, followed.
- **Cassandra and Ireena.** Back in the stockyard, Cassandra felt uneasy and asked Ireena's permission to hide her. With her Tarokka cards she **disguised herself as Ireena** (the Charlatan card) and **made the real Ireena invisible** (a card of a veiled woman). Ireena, Magda and Emeric stayed among the crates; the rest of the group trailed behind towards the church. Lyasra was startled to see "Ireena" talking with Cassandra's voice.
- **St. Andral's Church**, a short walk north-northeast of the inn: a slouching stone building with cracked windows, a heavy wooden door, a wrought-iron fence and a graveyard. Inside, singing and sobbing. The party heard the end of the congregation's prayer:

  > Saint Andral, Bringer of Light, Slayer of the Vampyr,
  > your bones are our walls, your grave our threshold.
  > Where you sleep, no devil may cross.
  > Keep the dark beyond the gate until the dawn.
  > All will be well.

- **Reyna's Divine Sense: the ground is hallowed.** She felt a warmth she hadn't felt anywhere in Barovia. While she held her focus **the warmth flickered, and for a moment wasn't there at all**, then returned.
- **Inside**, hundreds of men and women filled the pews, with no guards. **[Father Lucien](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md)** led the prayer from the lectern, with authority and kindness; an altar boy carried wine along the pews. **All the children sat in the centre pews.** Priest and altar boy wore the Morninglord's symbol: a gold sun-disc over a curved arc, ringed by rays, on purple. Lyasra froze on seeing the priest's vestments and was visibly on guard; the reason isn't known.
- "Ireena" (Cassandra) told Reyna off for greenhorn behaviour and told her to thank Lyasra. Reyna said she'd only been trying to get the child to shelter, that a church of the Light being steps away was a cruel irony, and needled the others about the autopsy. She introduced herself to the priest's side as a crusader of the Light come to "smash the necromancers, rescue some noble hostages, and stop the genocide of the Barovians."
- **[Yeska](../npcs/yeska.md)**, the altar boy, welcomed them: the Morninglord welcomes all who seek sanctuary, especially on a full moon. Frightened by the blood on Reyna's armour, he said she could speak to Father Lucien once the sermon was over, and took Gen to sleep with the other children ("the children always sleep in the middle, closest to the saint"). Father Lucien would pray all night; Yeska would wake them in the morning.
- Reyna checked the church's perimeter (no obvious weak points), sat on a front pew and bandaged her face. Before sleeping, she tried to **handcuff "Ireena" (really Cassandra) to a pew with the children**. Cassandra flatly refused: she'd stay in the middle of the group, but wouldn't make herself look any more out of place and suspicious than she already did. "Go guard the perimeter if that's really something you feel so strongly about."

**Key revelations & consequences:**

- **Reyna has lost an eye** to the clown at the Blue Water Inn.
- Reyna assaulted a guard of the watch and broke into the Blue Water Inn. Lyasra told the guard they were from Krezk. **The party then took refuge at St. Andral's in full view of a packed congregation.**
- St. Andral's is hallowed ground, the first Reyna has felt in Barovia, but the hallowing **flickered out for a moment** during the prayer.
- Vallaki at night: curfew, locked doors, garlic, a watch that greets people with "All will be well," and a Baron with "leashes for a full moon." The children are kept together at the heart of the church.
- Something is happening beyond the gate: fireball-like bursts, after the scream and lullaby heard there in Session 11.
- The real Ireena is invisible in the stockyard with Magda and Emeric; Cassandra is wearing her face inside the church, and refused Reyna's handcuffs.

**Key NPCs established/developed:**

- The clown at the Blue Water Inn: an acrobat with two hand crossbows who protects the inn. Not named to the party. *(GM-only: this is [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md) as Rictavio.)*
- The guard Reyna knocked out, left unconscious outside the inn. Not named to the party.
- [Father Lucien](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md) (seen, not spoken to) and [Yeska](../npcs/yeska.md), the altar boy.
- [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md): agreed to Cassandra's plan ("can't say anyone's ever wanted to impersonate me before").
