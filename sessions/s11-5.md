### Session 11.5 - Welcome to Vallaki

**Players:** [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md), [Lyasra](../pcs/lyasra.md) (Andreia); [Gaille](../pcs/gaille.md) (Dorothy), [Cassandra](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md) (Mary) and [Theren](../pcs/theren.md) (Tina) were tagged. [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) and [Gen](../npcs/gen.md) present as NPCs. **Channel:** Discord, from 2026-10-21.

**Context:** Online RP. It picks up the moment the party bribed the gate guards at the end of [Session 11](s11.md), before the scream, the fire and the lullaby heard at the gate.

*Partial log: this covers the RP up to the search for the inn (Discord messages through 2026-10-23). The rest (the Blue Water Inn, [Rictavio](../npcs/von-richten.md) and the guard, the refuge at St. Andral's) hasn't been added yet.*

**Summary:**

- The two guards counted the bribe and raised the gate. The one who took the coin, young and scrawny with patchy brown facial hair, had a hand crossbow strapped to his forearm under his cloak. He told the party to cross the ankle-deep stream between the gate and the town quickly, "else you'll be eatin' rot."
- As Lyasra crossed, that guard took out a scroll and charcoal and marked something on it. Lyasra noticed, and wondered whether it was some test for vampires.
- Inside, Vallaki was much bigger than Barovia. The party noted a path off the main road to a **stockyard** (crates, hay, animal pens); a flier on the eastern barracks reading "Blue Water Inn — The best wine in town!"; and, far down the main road, **a single steeple lit gold** above the rooftops.
- Reyna wanted to bribe an inn for its biggest room and "strap down the vampire bait." Lyasra mocked her for assuming money would open anything at this hour, and bristled at Reyna calling her "cleric" and treating her like an attendant.
- Out of the guards' earshot, Ireena suggested the large group hide in the stockyard while Reyna and Lyasra found lodging. Reyna proposed asking any inn for sanctuary ("either they give it and we pay, or they don't and odds are they're working for the necromancer"), and asked Ireena to cause a distraction for the guards. Ireena rolled her eyes ("not the first time I've been called a distraction"), agreed in exchange for the biggest bed at the inn, and went off to keep the guards busy.
- **Reyna and Lyasra went looking for the inn**, with Gen, who asked Lyasra to be her "buddy" because she doesn't like the dark. Reyna wanted Lyasra to pose as a nun with an orphan, guarded by Reyna; Lyasra pointed at her weapons and doubted anyone would believe it. Lyasra took Gen from Theren and lit a faint light on her amulet to comfort her. Everyone else hid in the stockyard.
- **Vallaki at night:** not a single window showed a candle, and several houses were barred shut. Lyasra followed her nose to the inn, and was taken aback by the smell of garlic everywhere. The flier gave no directions (Chris: "for a certain reason"), so Reyna headed for the nicer part of town, listening for drunken mirth.

**Key revelations & consequences:**

- Vallaki locks itself in at night: dark windows, barred doors, garlic everywhere.
- The young gate guard is armed beyond his station (a hidden hand crossbow) and keeps a written record of who enters.
- The Blue Water Inn's flier lacks directions on purpose; the GM hasn't said why.
- The party split: Reyna, Lyasra and Gen searched for the inn; the rest hid in the stockyard; Ireena was off distracting the guards.

**Key NPCs established/developed:**

- [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md): took charge of the group's safety and volunteered as the distraction.
- [Gen](../npcs/gen.md): bonded with Lyasra as her "buddy"; afraid of the dark.
- The young gate guard (unnamed).

**Open threads:**

- What did the guard write on his scroll, and for whom?
- Why does the Blue Water Inn's flier have no directions?
- The scream, fire and lullaby at the gate happen after this point. Was Ireena's distraction involved?
- The rest of the night: the Blue Water Inn, Rictavio, the dead guard, and the refuge at St. Andral's (described by the GMs, not yet logged).
