### Session 3

  - Players find pages of [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md)'s diary and [Gertruda](../npcs/gertruda-stiegler.md)'s Diary alongside the dead house Nori
      
      - Unknown to the players: Gertruda was attacked by werewolves. Von Richten saved her and is taking her to either his tower or Vallaki.
  - Players meet [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md) near Tser Falls
      
      - No players have eaten her dream pastries yet
  - Split the party
      
      - [Dismas](../npcs/dismas.md) and [Theren](../pcs/theren.md) decided to ambush the zombies before they got to Barovia
          
          - They survived by climbing a tree and successfully killed many zombies. Dismas saw [Rahadin](../npcs/rahadin.md) for the first time and recognized him from the locket.
      - The rest of the party rested and prepared for the siege
