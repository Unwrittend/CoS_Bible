### Session 12 - The Stolen Bones

**Players:** [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md), [Lyasra](../pcs/lyasra.md), [Cassandra](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md), [Theren](../pcs/theren.md), [Gaille](../pcs/gaille.md) (all players present)

**Context:** Not recorded. Logged from Chris's summary (2026-11-14). It picks up the morning after [Session 11.5](s11-5.md), at St. Andral's Church in Vallaki.

**Summary:**

- The party spoke with [Father Lucien](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md) at length.
- They learned that [Yeska](../npcs/yeska.md) had spoken a prayer aloud that revealed where St. Andral's bones were kept, and that [Milivoj](../npcs/milivoj.md) overheard it.
- **St. Andral's bones were stolen** from the church.
- **The Barovian refugee children were taken.** The party didn't learn where, beyond what [Cassandra](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md)'s divination spell showed:

  > You feel yourself take on an aspect of a raven when you channel the spell.
  > They march towards a den overlooking a mountain lake.

- The party went to Milivoj's home, met him and **bribed him**. They learned that **he had been hired by the coffin maker**. The coffin maker's name ([Henrik van der Voort](../npcs/henrik-van-der-voort.md)) wasn't given.
- [Lycius](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md), Cassandra's familiar, is now **addicted to [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)'s dream pies**.

**Key revelations & consequences:**

- St. Andral's Church has lost the bones that hallow it. The flicker in the hallowing that [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md) sensed in Session 11.5 is the likely sign the theft was under way.
- Yeska's prayer, not Milivoj's own knowledge, is how the hiding place of the bones got out.
- The theft has been traced to "the coffin maker."
- The refugee children are being marched to a den overlooking a mountain lake. The party doesn't know who took them. *(GM-only: werewolves, taking them to the werewolf den.)*

**Key NPCs established/developed:**

- [Father Lucien](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md): first conversation with the party.
- [Yeska](../npcs/yeska.md): his spoken prayer gave away where the bones were.
- [Milivoj](../npcs/milivoj.md): the gravedigger who stole the bones; met and bribed by the party. *(GM-only, GM-confirmed 2026-11-14: his brother [Dobri](../npcs/milivoj.md#dobri) is dead, killed by [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md); the method is deliberately left open.)*
- [Henrik van der Voort](../npcs/henrik-van-der-voort.md): known to the party only as "the coffin maker" who hired Milivoj.

**Open threads:**

- Recovering St. Andral's bones before they leave Vallaki.
- Finding the refugee children: a den overlooking a mountain lake.
- Lycius's addiction to the dream pies.
- [Arabelle](../npcs/arabelle.md) is still missing; the party doesn't know where she is. *(GM-only: she is in the coffin maker's house, where the bones trail now leads.)*
