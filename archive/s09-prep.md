# SECTION 13: SESSION 9 PREP REFERENCE — THE MAP, CAMPFIRE STORIES, THE PIE MILL & THE TSER FALLS ROC

**\[IDEA — NONCANONICAL, though this is the direct prep work behind the already-logged [Session 8](../sessions/s08.md)\]** Source: *Map Design Details for Players After Fortune Telling*. Kept here as a reference for the reasoning behind Session 8's beats, in case it needs to be revisited (e.g., if a player asks [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) a follow-up question about the map).

**[Session 9](../sessions/s09.md) update:** the campfire scene planned in 13.3 below was actually played out in Session 9 (see Session Log) — substantially as outlined for [Eliza](../npcs/eliza.md) and [Stanimir](../npcs/stanimir.md)'s stories, plus additional unplanned stories from [Arturi](../npcs/arturi.md) and [Gaille](../pcs/gaille.md). Ireena's own story slot was not used. The rest of this section (13.1, 13.2, 13.4, 13.5) remains GM prep reasoning, only partially touched by actual play so far — see the Section 5 Key Items updates for what's been confirmed.

## 13.1 — What Ireena can mark on the map

Deliberately budgeted so she isn't a walking answer key — one pinpoint location, one "somewhere in a specific building" location, and two "one of two possible sites" locations:

  

  - Werewolf den — vague, northwest Svalich Woods (unchanged).
  - [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md)'s flesh-map caches — flagged with X's; the actual flesh map is still a to-do item.
  - [Tome of Strahd](../lore/tome-of-strahd.md) — [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) has **no** location indicator; she doesn't know it exists.
  - [Sun Sword](../items.md#the-sun-sword) — tied to the Amber Temple, but Ireena doesn't know where the Temple is (capped at "not in one of the cities"); she **can** identify "the house of the fallen dragon" as the Order of the Silver Dragon/[Argonvost](../npcs/argonvost.md)'s Holt. **[Session 9](../sessions/s09.md) update:** she further narrowed this to an offshoot of the Luna River.
  - [Holy Symbol of Ravenkind](../items.md#the-holy-symbol--amulet-of-ravenkind) — narrowed to one of two candidate lakes, Lake Luna or Lake Baratok. **Session 9 update:** narrowed further, in play, to Luna Lake specifically.
  - A ghost-tied card — only "somewhere in Castle Ravenloft."

## 13.2 — Other quest hooks

  - Vallaki is [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md)'s personal destination.
  - **No hook yet exists** pointing toward the Abbey of [Saint Markovia](../npcs/saint-markovia.md) — a gap.
  - [Arabelle](../npcs/arabelle.md) (the doll) is tied to [Madam Eva](../npcs/madam-eva.md)'s fetch quest, in Vallaki.
  - "Dinner with the Devil" — the Castle Ravenloft dinner invitation, delivered as a fully optional invite.

## 13.3 — The campfire storytelling scene

  - [Arturi](../npcs/arturi.md)'s story — a metaphorical tale about an old fox being hunted; a coded retelling of [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md)'s hunt. Flavor only. **[Session 9](../sessions/s09.md) update:** told in play as a fox/sparrows/rats revenge fable — a high insight check registered genuine emotional intensity behind it, so this may carry more real narrative weight than "flavor only."
  - [Eliza](../npcs/eliza.md)'s raw-module [Lugdana](../npcs/lugdana.md)/Ravenkind verse — a rework tying it to Lugdana's Fane-restoration attempt was considered and **set aside**; didn't produce a real hook and didn't suit Eliza's characterization.
  - [Stanimir](../npcs/stanimir.md)'s raw-module "Vistani can leave Barovia" parable is **reassigned to Eliza**. **Session 9 update:** told in play as the Vistani founding parable (the wounded "prince"); judged true by most listeners.
  - Stanimir, freed up, tells the **Lugdana story** instead — framed as a tale about "his best friend," gesturing toward Yester Hill without a concrete reason to go there yet. **Session 9 update:** told in play; judged a half-truth ("Lugdana surely existed... we do not know where she is").
  - [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) keeps her own story — the vehicle for her wolf-chase "nightmare"/blessing memory (see [Section 10.4](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#104--ireenas-anti-curse-blessing-major-plot-device)). **Session 9 update:** not used this session — still pending for a future session.
  - Stanimir is placed last as a pacing safety valve — can skip his turn if the table's energy flags.
  - Presentation plan: all four stories trimmed shorter; players choose to roleplay or have the GMs summarize (Inspiration reward for participating). Doubles as an in-character "quest recap" via Ireena marking up the map, with an out-of-character bullet-list fallback. Drafted line for Ireena on [Gertruda](../npcs/gertruda-stiegler.md): wouldn't call it a "quest," takes [Mad Mary](../npcs/mad-mary.md)'s account with a grain of salt, but is genuinely worried and hopes Gertruda might be among the Vallaki refugees.

## 13.4 — The Pie Mill (reworked Old Durst Mill)

The old Durst family mill, now run by [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md) and her daughters/sisters as a "confectionery" selling cheap, popular pies (~1 gp/box of six, first-time half-price discount). Deliberately **not** introduced as "the creepy old mill" — [Eliza](../npcs/eliza.md) recommends it warmly as a wholesome local landmark; [Stanimir](../npcs/stanimir.md) is more cautious/wary, a deliberate contrast.

  

**Content note, handle with care:** the mill's "bloodberry pies" were workshopped with a secret ingredient of menstrual blood harvested from the adolescent girls working there (kept complacent/drugged), played as grim dark-comedy about exploitative labor in a resource-scarce land — in the same body-horror/hag-lore register as the published Morgantha dream-pastry hook, not romantic or sexual content. **This needs a deliberate, separate decision from you** about how much of it, if any, actually surfaces at the table versus staying GM-only flavor.

## 13.5 — The Tser Falls bridge: Strahd near-miss and the Roc

  - Logistics behind the already-logged [Session 8](../sessions/s08.md) near-miss: [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) was nearby (plausibly getting his own fortune read by [Madam Eva](../npcs/madam-eva.md)) and is heading back to Castle Ravenloft by carriage as the party arrives via a different route — hence the Strix delegation inviting the party to dinner rather than Strahd appearing in person. [Rahadin](../npcs/rahadin.md) delivering the invitation personally was explicitly rejected as reading like a threatening summons rather than a genuine invitation.
  - **New monster idea, inspired by (not adopted from) a third-party homebrew "Rock of Mount Gakis" writeup:** a single powerful bird-monster for this region (explicitly not two). Leaning toward it being **Madam Eva's Roc**, corrupted/subjugated by Strahd (see [Section 4](../npcs/index.md), Strahd and Madam Eva entries) rather than an unrelated Strahd-owned creature — keeping the bird motif tied to the Autumn Fane. If a second Roc/nest exists, a location was floated at **"Mount Berrypuck,"** doubling as a flesh-map cache. Smaller falconry-themed alternatives (giant bat, "Nightwing" bat, unspecified falcon) were considered as less-lethal substitutes if a full Roc is too strong for the party's level — **undecided**. Tone goal: a genuine, non-Strahd-specific danger the party can hide from or barely survive, not necessarily win outright. A gentler alternative (Strahd glimpsed feeding his pet Roc near the bridge, non-combat) was also floated and left open. **2026-09-15 update:** the Roc of Mount Gakis is now named as the Autumn Fane's **primordial beast** ([Section 15.1](s10-prep.md#151--the-primordial-beasts)).
  - Unresolved philosophical debate: Chris prefers hand-placed, semi-scripted location encounters; Michelle prefers true random encounters for a more organic feel. Used the scripted approach for this specific bridge scene; the general default wasn't resolved.
