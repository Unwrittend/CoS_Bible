# SECTION 15: SESSION 10 PREP — LAKE ZAROVICH, THE WINTER WORM, THE WITHERING SICKNESS & THE VALLAKI APPROACH

**\[IDEA — NONCANONICAL\] Everything in this section is GM ideation, not actual play.** Sources (in chronological order — where they disagree, the later session is treated as the current preferred version and the disagreement is flagged): *Vallaki Major Players: Valochians, Watchers, Mardikovs, and More* (2026-09-15); *Primordial Beasts and Fanes: Setting Up Lake Zarovich Lore* (2026-09-15); *Preparing Encounters for Vallaki Session with [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)* (2026-09-19).

**Design guardrail raised during these sessions:** Chris flagged that the more the Fane/worm lore grows, the more the campaign risks becoming "a Fane storyline with [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) as a side character." The resolution reached on 2026-09-19 is a layered structure — **schemes within schemes**: the Vallaki politics (Volokoviches, Watchers) are the simple, visible "small fries" the players engage with first; behind them sits Strahd's actual machination (15.4), which the players only gradually discover was driving events all along. Michelle's framing: the GMs have all the pieces and no picture; the players have the picture ("Strahd is the enemy") and none of the pieces. **Arc framing:** "Phase one is get the [Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness)." What was originally planned as a Tome-of-Strahd / Festival-of-the-Blazing-Sun season finale is now leaning toward a Lake Zarovich "scuba dive" boss fight against the Winter Worm.

## 15.2 — The Winter Worm & the Heart of Darkness

  - The Winter Worm sleeps at the bottom of **Lake Zarovich** beside (or holding — see below) the **[Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness)**. The Winter Fane's tower/presence there predates Saint Andral.
  - **Whirlpools:** the worm "yawning" in its sleep creates great whirlpools. Earlier framing (2026-09-15): whirlpools appear periodically during certain lunar/solar events, when the seal on the Heart of Darkness weakens. The two framings are compatible — a weakening seal is when the worm stirs enough to yawn.
  - **Why it's stirring now:** something [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) has done since his reawakening (~one month ago in-world) — his efforts to find/reach the Winter Fane, and specifically the long-running poisoning campaign (15.4) — is weakening the seal and waking the worm. **The [Void Child](../npcs/khirad.md)'s proximity alone would not wake anything**; only a real triggering event (e.g. a Winter-Fane-linked object actually going into the water) would.
  - **The worm is lonely,** in keeping with the Winter Fane's restoration condition ("friendship / no longer alone," [Section 9.3](../lore/cosmology.md#93--restoration-conditions)). In the late stages of infection its parasites may transmit that loneliness to their hosts (15.3).
  - **Heart of Darkness — unresolved details:**
      - 2026-09-15: Michelle suggested the Heart is some manifestation of power **held within the worm**. 2026-09-19: the working version is that it's a **relic at the lake bottom guarded by the worm**, and Strahd must **kill the worm** to take it. Not reconciled — both are live.
      - Is it one stone or several? Floated: one great stone that, if broken, "comets out into the sky" and scatters like seeds for the party to hunt down; or it's the strongest of the Fanes' powers; or it's the piece needed to bring the others together; or it splits into three. **Undecided.**
      - Floated as a possible vehicle for introducing the Dark Powers' corruption. Not developed.
  - **Folk knowledge:** because the parasite outbreak is new (15.3), Vallaki has little folk knowledge of anything wrong in the lake. The whirlpools, though, are older and sporadic. People would know they happen but not why.

## 15.3 — The Withering Sickness (parasites + poison)

  - **Parasites:** small worm-like parasites are pieces sloughing off the waking Winter Worm. They were dormant until [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s return and have only appeared in roughly the last month. Naming: the GMs settled on **"the Winter Worm" for the single giant beast and just "parasites" for the small ones**. ("Winter worms" for the parasites and "great worm" for the beast were both considered and dropped. The spelling "wyrm" was also floated.)
  - **Transmission is by drinking the water, not eating the fish.** Fish get cooked; untreated lake water doesn't. **Vallaki draws from wells, which is why the town itself is largely safe;** the Barovian refugees camped outside drink straight from Lake Zarovich.
  - **Water flow (geography, important):** water runs **Luna Lake → Luna River → Lake Zarovich → onward toward Mount Baratok.** Parasites spread downstream of the lake, away from the upstream Vistani camp. Strahd's poison (15.4) enters **upstream**, so it reaches everyone downstream of its source.
  - **Who is sick with what:**
      - **Barovian refugees:** poison **and** parasites — the worst off.
      - **Vistani camp near Vallaki:** poison only (upstream of the parasites). They noticed they were getting sick, switched to *purify food and drink*, and improved, but that spell only scales to a small group. They don't blame the water. They blame the Barovians: "ever since they started storming Vallaki they have brought disease and famine." They have also noticed animals dying.
      - **Vallakians:** mostly protected by the wells. (Earlier idea: poorer fish-eating commoners get sick more than the wealthy. Superseded by the wells/drinking framing but compatible with it.)
  - **Symptoms ("withering sickness"):** appetite and satiety changes, constant hunger and thirst, anemia, bruxism, digestive trouble, fatigue, joint and muscle pain, skin issues, sleep problems, swelling, and progressive malnourishment/wasting. It also brings **depression/hopelessness**, fitting the Winter Fane's theme. People are dying in droves. **Full life-cycle stages are still TBD** — the plan is to base them on real parasitism.
  - **Late-stage hive-mind (floated):** advanced parasites may relay the worm's feelings (its loneliness) to their hosts and compel them to bring things (people) to the lake (see [Arabelle](../npcs/arabelle.md), 15.8).
  - **Rejected/narrowed:** an earlier (2026-09-15) idea that infection targets only people with a specific trait (hopelessness) was floated. It was noted that this would infect a lot of Barovians. It could still explain why [Vargas](../npcs/baron-vargas-volokovich.md) insists everyone be happy — "unhappy people fall into despair and become prone to infection." The later drinking-water model replaced it as the mechanism, though despair remains thematically tied in.
  - **[Lyasra](../pcs/lyasra.md)'s *purify food and drink* is now story-relevant** (Michelle's observation).
  - **Lycanthropy cut from the refugee camp for scope:** some refugees have been bitten on the road, but none of the bitten remain in the camp now (Vargas has them lynched on sight: "Werewolves are the servants of Strahd, and we do not stand Strahd in this house"). The refugees still report werewolf attacks on the road and may ask the party for protection.

## 15.4 — Strahd's Machination: Poisoning the Winter Worm

**This is the "action [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) is taking" the GMs had been missing.**

  - **Goal:** obtain the [Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness). Strahd can't enter running water, and the worm is too dangerous to fight awake, so he is **poisoning it to death slowly** and making the lake uninhabitable for it.
  - **Who runs it:** **[Ludmilla](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic)** (Strahd's first/eldest bride, Ludmilla Vilisevic — [Section 4](../npcs/index.md); canonically the vampire the party drove off in [Session 9](../sessions/s09.md)) has made this her sole long-term task **since [Lugdana](../npcs/lugdana.md)'s era** (Strahd saw that the Heart might be an alternative power source after that defeat). While everyone watches Strahd, no one watches what Ludmilla is doing. The idea of starting this as far back as [Saint Markovia](../npcs/saint-markovia.md)'s era was considered and dropped in favor of Lugdana's era. Escher was ruled out as too recent.
  - **The poison** (working name "Luminos poison," as transcribed) is crafted **with [Baba Lysaga](../npcs/baba-lysaga.md)'s help**, and possibly with **[Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)** involved. It is magical in nature: acidic and cell-destroying, eating at the worm's exoskeleton (Michelle's image: a cell membrane breaking down, or poisoned condors). It also has a **sedative** quality that keeps the worm from waking while it dies slowly, so Strahd risks less.
      - **Ingredient:** **cat-o'-nine-tails** (cattails), the sedative component, native to the area around Mount Gakis. Morgantha was already shown collecting it in play.
      - **Morgantha's dream pastries are a heavily weakened, non-lethal version of this same poison** — the sedative in a dose that doesn't kill. This gives the players a way to connect the pastries to the larger scheme.
  - **Source and delivery:** the "plant" is **upstream, on the Luna River / at Luna Lake** — factories with toxic runoff feeding into Lake Zarovich. The GMs' shorthand for Strahd here was "an oil company."
  - **Dosage escalation:** up to now Strahd has been running low-dose tests, which is why *purify food and drink* still works and people survive. **Over the ~4–5 days the party is likely to spend in Vallaki, he raises the dose and things get much worse.**
  - **Ties to actual play (Session 9), as ideation:**
      - **The erratic owlbear and vultures at the Tser Falls bridge** are proposed as poison runoff victims — animals dying downstream (a California-condor-style poisoning).
      - **The [Woodcutter's Bride](../npcs/woodcutters-bride.md)'s lumber operation** (canonically Ludmilla's — the same bride who runs this poisoning): proposed as **two undead supply lines** — one to Castle Ravenloft (for the Roc) and one to the poison works. This recasts part of what she was building.
  - **Retrieval plan:** the Heart lies at the bottom of running water Strahd can't enter. Draining the lake or diverting the river (Netherlands-style) was considered and rejected — it would take too long, and Vallaki would notice. **[Rahadin](../npcs/rahadin.md) — not a vampire — leads the dive,** supported by undead that don't need to breathe and under a water-breathing / air-bubble effect. Strahd trusting Rahadin with this, and Rahadin simply doing it, is meant as a powerful on-screen demonstration of his loyalty. Chris's reference image: deep-sea saturation divers repairing pipelines. Strahd and Ludmilla may concentrate on the sustaining spell above.
  - **Strahd's inner circle:** **Strahd, Rahadin, and Ludmilla** form a tight trio who know the real plan (see 15.5 for [Anastrasia](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#anastrasya-karelova) and [Volenta](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#volenta-popofsky)).

## 15.5 — Strahd's Offer to the Party, the Bones of Saint Andral & the Brides

  - **[Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s pitch (if the party ever accepts his invitation):** *"I have discovered what has been poisoning the people of Vallaki — a giant worm at the bottom of Lake Zarovich. Its taint is spreading. As Lord of Barovia I have a responsibility to remove its foul presence. I am putting together a task force with [Rahadin](../npcs/rahadin.md) at its head. You have proven yourselves effective… I would like your assistance, for a favor of your choosing."* Sweeteners: he will allow them to leave his domain, and he will open his vaults and fashion or grant a magic item of their choosing. All of it is **sealed by a magical contract**. This refines the existing [Section 10.5](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#105--faction-geography--the-heart-of-darkness) fetch-quest/player-exit bargain rather than replacing it.
  - **Debrief:** Strahd would brief them on the terrain — a relic at the bottom of the lake, guarded by a giant worm that is dormant and slumbering. They are **not to wake it** and are to kill it while it sleeps.
  - **If the party refuses,** the werewolves go down with Rahadin instead.
  - **The encounter is deliberately stacked in Strahd's favor.** It must be possible for the players to win, but it's designed to be excessively hard — the genuinely difficult boss fight Chris wants for them.
      - Rahadin carries a **contingency teleport**: if the party turns on him, he's whisked to safety. Once the worm dies, he teleports out with the worm's body, and **the party's air bubble vanishes** — leaving them under a great depth of water to solve that on their own.
      - **Reward cleverness (Andrea's advice):** if the players act smartly, they can keep a portion of the worm's body for their own investigation or crafted equipment, at the cost of the air-bubble problem above.
      - **Fire underwater:** Chris wants fire spells and fire damage to simply not work in this encounter, and wants the players told in advance. Michelle asked to hold off on mechanical decisions for now. **Unresolved.**
  - **Earlier dinner idea (2026-09-15), now superseded:** at dinner, Strahd would warn the party that someone is going to steal Saint Andral's bones from Vallaki, while knowing his bride [Anastrasia](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#anastrasya-karelova) had been tasked with exactly that — a test or a bit of fun, possibly with the Festival of the Blazing Sun as his pretext to storm Vallaki if she succeeds. **Chris found the logical thread weak** ("doesn't sound like our Strahd would care for that") and noted the players likely won't attend dinner anyway. The Heart-of-Darkness task force above is now the preferred Strahd offer. (A "wine fetch quest" was half-remembered from early ideation. It's the same wine/[Mardikov](../npcs/the-martikov-family.md) hook already in [Section 4](../npcs/index.md), predating the lake plan.)
  - **Saint Andral's bones (current version):** the bones keep the ground inside Vallaki consecrated. Floated: **that consecration is what keeps the poison out of Vallaki's wells** — so stealing the bones would expose the town. **Anastrasia's bones heist is her own initiative** to ingratiate herself with Strahd. He simply allows it ("I'd love to be able to go into Vallaki, so go ahead"). She **doesn't know the grand plan**.
  - **[Ludmilla](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic) vs. Anastrasia:** Ludmilla dislikes Anastrasia's indiscreet methods, which risk getting in the way of the real mission. She still recognizes their value as a distraction providing cover for it. This is the brides' built-in friction.
  - **[Volenta Popofsky](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#volenta-popofsky)** (transcribed "Violetta"; confirmed 2026-09-24 to be the same character) is "the berserker." Where she fits is still undecided.
  - **Escher** attaches himself to Volenta wherever he can, believing he can manipulate her and trying to make himself useful.

## 15.6 — Vallaki's Factions (current working model)

The major players named: the **Volokoviches**, the **Watcher House**, the **Mardikovs** (not discussed further), **Saint Andral's priest ([Father Lucien Petrovich](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md))**, the **Vistani** outside the walls, and a small group of **male Dusk Elves** with one important leader ([Kasimir](../npcs/kasimir-velikov.md)).

  - **The Volokoviches:**
      - **Goal:** keep [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) out, first and foremost, and maintain the peace — which in their minds means making sure everyone *seems* happy.
      - **Inciting incident for the festivals:** Strahd has just woken up (~a month ago). The ancestral enemy is back; Vallaki believes itself safe, but "to truly be safe" the festivals of happiness must keep him out.
      - **[Baron Vargas Volokovich](../npcs/baron-vargas-volokovich.md) wants the power inside Lake Zarovich** to fight Strahd's coming onslaught. Michelle noted that this runs directly against what Saint Andral would want. How Vargas knows anything about the lake:
          - **[Izek](../npcs/izek-strazani.md)'s childhood story** — he kept insisting there was a monster in the lake, and only Vargas believed him.
          - Possibly Vargas's own sight of the worm.
          - Floated: noticing the infection among refugees and linking it back to what happened to Izek.
      - **Paranoia:** Vargas sees assassins in every shadow and blames Strahd for "poisoning the water we drink" — ironically, he's right.
      - **Why Barovians are refused entry:**
          - They bring **disease and unhappiness**, and unhappiness attracts maladies and Strahd.
          - Vargas also suspects werewolves among them.
          - Floated: Vallaki *did* accept Barovians at first, until they brought disease with them.
  - **The Watcher House** (use "House," per the book's theming):
      - **Goal:** re-establish the family's power and standing, by either
          - restoring **[Stella](../npcs/stella-watcher.md)** from madness, or
          - positioning the House to survive Strahd's new era — currying his favor, or taking ownership of the town.
      - The players could potentially help cure Stella, which would complete [Fiona](../npcs/lady-fiona-watcher.md)'s goal.
      - **Means under consideration (not settled):**
          - Fiona reaching out to one of [Strahd's brides](../npcs/strahds-brides.md) — most likely **[Ludmilla](../npcs/strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic)**, known to be a mage — to cure Stella.
          - Or reaching **[Baba Lysaga](../npcs/baba-lysaga.md)**. Folk tales cast her as a great healer whose cures come at a great price, and she'd use a pseudonym. Fiona couldn't contact her directly, only through a coven member.
          - It's **unresolved** whether Baba Lysaga has covens/agents at all. Chris imagined her acquiring agents; Michelle had pictured a lone operator. Nothing in the book suggests Berez is inhabited.
          - Fiona may have learned her cult practices from such a coven.
      - Her imp familiar is self-taught occultism; you don't need to be a high-level caster for it.
      - **Family:**
          - The husband, **Nikolai**, died of sickness three years ago; his corpse rots in Fiona's bedroom. (Chris had been picturing him gone about a year.) Their son **Nikolai II** is named after him (confirmed 2026-09-24).
          - The two sons have none of their mother's ambition or temper. They spend her money, drinking and partying to escape the loss of their sister, and think their mother has gone a little mad.
          - The family was once tight-knit; loss has broken it.
          - Stella went mad after her marriage into the Volokovich family.
  - **Father Lucien Petrovich (St. Andral's)** and his altar boy **Yeska** (the grave-digger is Milivoj, not present) work among the refugees, trying to heal the sick with holy power. Lucien doesn't know what the malady is, but sees people dying in droves.
  - **Dusk Elves:** a small group of male Dusk Elves, with **Kasimir** as the important one. "Help from the Dusk Elves" was pencilled in as a possible way into Vallaki, pending the Vistani camp work.

## 15.8 — Arabelle (undecided)

**[Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) update (canonical):** [Arabelle](../npcs/arabelle.md) went missing in the Vallaki marketplace around 9 a.m. on her 7th birthday. The options below now describe *who took her and where she is*, not where she starts.

The GMs deliberately didn't commit to where Arabelle is. Michelle's point: decide *what she's for* before deciding where she is. Options on the table:

  - **Lake Zarovich (RAW-adjacent)**, thrown in as "fish bait," but reframed:
      - the man in the boat is a late-stage parasite host, compelled by the worm's **loneliness** to bring it a "playmate";
      - **or** it's one of the forest druids, infected and convinced the Fanes/"sisters" are lonely and that this is as natural as breathing — which would be the players' first introduction to the "weird druids."
      - **Donavich was rejected** as the abductor: the parasite couldn't have taken hold that fast.
  - **With the Vistani camp**, to avoid adding more threads.
  - **DragnaCarta options:** Escher searching for her as a sacrifice to the mists; or she's rescued by [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md) and kept in his carnival wagon with the tiger.

## 15.9 — Strahd's Own Tarokka Reading (ideation draft, not canon)

### A. Road to Vallaki — Morgantha & Elaine

**Characters:**

  - **[Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md).** Her daughters are spelled **Bella Sunbane** and **Offelia Wormwiggle** in the prep doc.
  - **[Elaine Groze](../npcs/elaine-groze.md)**, Morgantha's "granddaughter." The prep doc spells her name "Elllaine," which could be a typo for Elaine or Ellaine; this bible keeps "Elaine" until you settle it. This gives her the surname the 09-19 session left TBD. Her cue: the sound of laughter.

**Divine Sense read-aloud** (for a PC with Divine Sense):

  - *Morgantha:* "The stout, rosy-cheeked saturates to an unnatural black and white. The shadows crawl underneath the old woman's being, creating contrasts so strong it's difficult for you to identify it's 'human' features. A hunger not of your body gnaws at you. Where it stands, the radiance within you dims and leans away. It is fiendish. This much is certain. Its nature is somewhere below, far from any mortal sin."
  - *The child:* "The child beside her is a bright beacon, as any unsullied soul is, and the darkness licks at her skin as well. Where the darkness seeps, the light seems to dim."
  - **Note:** this reads Elaine as an **unsullied mortal child** tainted by proximity to Morgantha — not a hag or hag-spawn. That settles the 09-19 question about whether she's blood kin, leaning toward "not related."

**[Stella](../npcs/stella-watcher.md)'s diary beat (supersedes the 15.7A draft):**

  - Elaine reads aloud: *"… Oh, how would [Victor](../npcs/victor-volokovich.md) ever love me. The mirror reflects my sallow skin, and the veins protruding from my eyes un-undu-undulate with worms! He can't see me like this. I must claw them out!"*
  - *(She stops for laughter.)*
  - **Morgantha:** *"Did Stella claw those pretty little peepers out?"*
  - **Elaine:** *"Hmm… The entries stop here, Grand mama."*

**Situation:**

  - The two are coming back from selling pies and goods to the Barovian refugees.
  - A few pies are left in the cart: a **meat pie** and a **vegetable cheese pie**, **1 gp each**.
  - Morgantha's sales pitch: *"Cheapest food you'll find outside of these gates. Those damned profiteering Vallakian vultures are robbing the poor Barovians for every copper to their name!"*
  - She also sells an **"all natural herbal remedy — purified valerian extract."** Valerian is one of the best-known natural sedatives. Wild valerian thrives in damp meadows and along the edges of rivers and streams, and its root has been used for centuries to calm the nerves and induce sleep.
      - **REVISION flag:** 15.4 names **cat-o'-nine-tails** as the poison's sedative ingredient. It's undecided whether valerian replaces cattails, adds to them, or is only Morgantha's public cover product. Either way, a riverside sedative fits the Luna River poison works nicely. See [Section 14](../open-questions.md).
  - She sells at dawn **every other day** (15.7A said "at daybreak").

**Information she offers:**

  - The gates of Vallaki are closed to all outsiders, but they **might open to visitors with coin for the Festival of the Blazing Sun**: *"We're preparing for a large feast then you know, in three day's time."* That puts the festival **three days** out.
  - **If the party buys a pie**, she's friendlier and shares more: *"If you want into Vallaki, you'll need to get past the fool [Vargas](../npcs/baron-vargas-volokovich.md)' attack dog, [Izek](../npcs/izek-strazani.md). He's been the one keeping the Barovians out."*
      - **REVISION flag:** the 09-19 session moved this Izek warning *away* from Morgantha, because she had no reason to volunteer it. The prep doc puts it back, but only after a purchase, which gives her a transactional motive. **The prep doc is the current version.**

### B. Vallaki Gates — the Refugee Camp

**Read-aloud (camp):**

*"Past Vallaki's gates lies a cluster of ramshackle tents huddled against the wall, as though seeking shelter beneath the looming silhouette of the town's palisade walls. The tents themselves are laid out in a simple two-sided thoroughfare, with crude wooden barricades and muddy ditches beyond the far side facing out against the dark forest beyond.*

*Through gaps in the threadbare canvas, you can see crude wooden pallets, makeshift bedrolls, and the occasional battered cooking pot. Small, low-burning cookfires dot the grimy narrow avenue, and piles of sticks and half-rotted logs rest haphazardly beside them.*

*Dozens of people, their bodies gaunt and their faces drawn, move slowly about the camp. Their eyes are sunken and hollow, their clothes hanging loose on their thin frames. A few of them huddle about the campfires, bodies hunched against the chill air and hands outstretched for the meager warmth."*

**New NPCs:**

  - **Emeric**, a melancholy older man with graying hair.
  - **Magda**, a grief-stricken young woman with deep circles under her eyes.
  - Both came with the first wave of refugees from Barovia. They have led the effort to welcome and organize newcomers since [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s siege.
  - They're wary of outsiders but curious about why the party has come.
  - Magda briefly hopes the party are Baron Vallakovich's servants, come to invite the refugees in. Emeric points to the party's strange appearance and sets her straight.

**What Emeric and Magda can share** (adjust to current events):

  - **When the refugees arrived:** the first ones came **three months ago** and were barred from entering. When they tried to force the gates, the guards summoned **[Izek](../npcs/izek-strazani.md)**, *"who bore a twisted devil's arm and conjured fire to drive the refugees back."*
  - **Life in the camp:**
      - They've **bribed the guards** for small necessities like the tents.
      - They can't get in, and can't get the Baron to hear them.
  - **Nightly attacks:**
      - **Swarms of bats and packs of wolves** have hit the camp every night.
      - *"Thankfully, no one has died yet, but several refugees have been wounded."*
  - **Why they stay:** after their losses on the road, and with the Devil in Castle Ravenloft, they won't risk going home. They're waiting for the Baron to relent.
  - **Dream pastries:**
      - About **one quarter** of the refugees are addicted to [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)'s pastries, as an escape from their misery.
      - Eating one causes a trance, per the module's *Dream Pastries* (p. 125).
  - **Franz:**
      - If the party asks about the night hag, Emeric and Magda exchange glances. Her description matches a recurring nightmare that **Franz** has had for the past two nights.
      - Franz is a widower who recently lost his children as well. He is likely still grieving or disturbed.
      - Magda adds that he has had **a strange wasting disease ever since the nightmares began**, and hopes the party can treat him.
  - **[Father Lucien](../npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md)** of Saint Andral's church has been tending the wounded. *"Pious man he may be, there is no healing, just a suspension from death."*

**Franz's tent (read-aloud):**

*"Within this sagging, cramped tent lie three stained pallets, two of which are child-sized. An old and patched stuffed rabbit lies across the closest pallet, its black button eyes staring sightlessly into the air.*

*A young woman kneels in the mud beside the full-size pallet, clutching a bundle of wilted herbs in one fist and holding a damp rag over the forehead of a frail, gaunt-looking man lying across the pallet. The man's skin is pale and drenched in sweat, his clothes torn and ragged. Wispy, scraggled hair rises from his wrinkled scalp, while his face, illuminated beneath the dying light of a single, sputtering candle, is etched with exhaustion. A full moment passes before you realize that this ghost of a man is likely scarcely older than thirty."*

  - The young woman at Franz's side is unnamed.
  - **Open design question:** is Franz's wasting from the hag's nightmares, from the withering sickness (15.3), or both? A night hag feeding on a dream pastry addict would give the sickness a second, overlapping cause worth deciding on. See [Section 14](../open-questions.md).

**Lucien's entrance (read-aloud fragment):**

*"Emeric and Magda speaking to a man with worn robes that engulf a lithe frame. You see the crest of the Morninglord emblazoned on his lapels. There are deep hollow under his eyes, and at his side you see a similarly dressing young boy. They both look much cleaner than the Barovians."*

The boy is presumably Yeska.
