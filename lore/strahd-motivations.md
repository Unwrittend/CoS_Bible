# SECTION 10: STRAHD'S MOTIVATIONS, THE THREE-WAY POWER STRUGGLE & FACTION GEOGRAPHY

**\[IDEA — NONCANONICAL\]** Sources: *[Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s Motivations, Goals and Character Development Discussion*; *Strahd's Fate: Mastering Destiny Against Dark Powers*; *[Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md)'s Darkening Path and Faction Power Struggles*.

## 10.1 — Strahd's goals/motivations table

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| **Goal** | **Underlying motivation** |
| Take revenge ([Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md)'s family/Kolyan's line, Doru, Ismark's line; Mordakovs standing, not finalized) | Revenge, full stop |
| Obtain more power | Refusal to be controlled by anyone — "master of his own fate" |
| Escape Barovia | Also tied to mastery of his own fate |
| Claim Ireena's soul | Belief that he owns Tatiana / is entitled to her |

  

Cross-cutting: exerting dominance/superiority (revenge + claiming Ireena); entertainment (revenge + claiming/toying with Ireena + spy network). Character traits kept distinct from motivations: egocentric, prideful, cunning, and a polite "gentlemanly facade" masking the cunning.

  

**Cleaner restated model** (from *[Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s Fate*): Strahd has exactly two root motivations — (1) mastery of his own fate/destiny (freedom from the Dark Powers), and (2) a genuine love of entertainment/spectacle. Every other goal is downstream of one or both.

## 10.3 — The three-way power struggle: Dark Powers vs. Strahd vs. the Fanes

  - **Dark Powers** → tied to the Amber Temple and [Void Child](../npcs/khirad.md).
  - **[Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)** → led by Strahd, with [Rahadin](../npcs/rahadin.md) as his primary agent.
  - **The Fanes** → led by [Madam Eva](../npcs/madam-eva.md), with the [Mardikov](../npcs/the-martikov-family.md) family tied to this faction (exact role unspecified).
  - **Explicitly excluded** from this faction map: player characters, including [Cassandra](../pcs/cassandra-priam.md) — this is a map of the world's own power players, not party affiliations.
  - **[Baba Lysaga](../npcs/baba-lysaga.md)** occupies a dual position — tied to Strahd directly, with acknowledged (unresolved) connections to the Dark Powers as well.
  - **Floated, explicitly NOT canonized:** Rahadin striking a deal with the Dark Powers to resurrect Strahd each time he dies. A possible throughline, deliberately left uncanonized.
  - **Confirmed within the ideation sessions** (still unconfirmed at the table): Rahadin has sacrificed the Dusk Elves for some purpose — whether for the Dark Powers or his own ends is undetermined, but the event itself is treated as settled.
  - **The Winter Fane is the connective tissue** tying all three factions together — already bound to the Dark Powers, with Strahd trying to seize/control her via the [Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness), while the surviving Fanes presumably want her back. Whichever faction wins her gains a decisive advantage — **what that advantage is was explicitly left open.**
  - **[Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md)/Tatiana's "claiming" reframed as a Trojan horse:** Strahd's visible fixation on winning/claiming Ireena functions partly as a smokescreen — the Dark Powers assume his "game" is just winning her, so they intervene less in the schemes that actually matter to his real goal of breaking free from them.

## 10.4 — Ireena's anti-curse blessing (major plot device)

[Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) was blessed with some form of anti-curse protection while alone in the woods, before Kolyan found her — most likely by the **Summer Fane**, though a joint Summer+Autumn blessing was also floated. When [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) bit her, she didn't turn — the first time in any of Tatiana's incarnations his usual method (bite → vampire spawn → obedient companion) has failed. This shifts Strahd from default domination into genuine curiosity and a **carrot-and-stick courtship**: sincere attempts to win her affection alternating with escalating psychological/physical coercion once charm-based domination fails too.

  

  - **Possible seeding beat:** Ireena recounts what she believes is a recurring nightmare of being chased through the forest by a large black wolf (the Summer Fane's animal) — with the twist, left for you to reveal when ready, that this "nightmare" is actually a suppressed memory of the real blessing.
  - The blessing's scope is broad/flexible — a general "anti-curse mechanism" rather than vampire-immunity specifically, potentially covering lycanthropy and Vistani curses too; sufficiently powerful Fane-level entities could still overcome it via a contested roll.
  - **Tabled, not resolved:** whether a mortal's soul actually departs the body upon vampire-spawn conversion, or remains present in diminished form — raised as a possible explanation for why Tatiana keeps reincarnating even after past spawn-conversions.
  - **Direct consequence:** [Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md) becomes specifically fascinated by Ireena the moment he learns she can resist vampirism, and wants to experiment on her — another symptom of his darkening arc (see his NPC entry, [Section 4](../npcs/index.md)). The Dark Powers don't control him yet; they find him increasingly *enticing* as a potential asset given his own moral decay.

## 10.5 — Faction geography & the Heart of Darkness

  - A physical faction map exercise placed [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md), the Fanes, [Baba Lysaga](../npcs/baba-lysaga.md) (Berez), the Mardikovs (Wizard of Wines, near/in Vallaki), the Dark Powers generally, the werewolves, and flesh-map undead-cache locations. **Kresk and (implicitly) [Mardikov](../npcs/the-martikov-family.md) territory are the only two fully unconquered "anti-Strahd" bastions** left in Barovia.
  - **Floated, not committed:** the village of Barovia itself could eventually be fully absorbed into Strahd's own military staging ground later in the campaign, possibly via a future attack forcing the party to defend or retake it. **[Session 9](../sessions/s09.md) note:** the party's discovery of a noble vampire ("the [Woodcutter's Bride](../npcs/woodcutters-bride.md)," [Section 4](../npcs/index.md)) coordinating a castle-scale undead lumber-gathering operation is the first actual-play evidence pointing in this direction — still not confirmed as the same plan, but worth revisiting this floated idea in light of it.
  - **The [Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness)** (the Winter Fane's remaining essence) was tentatively placed at **Lake Zarovich** (a mountain location was the other candidate), with an unconfirmed thematic justification — that the Winter Fane may have drowned herself there out of sorrow. **2026-09-19 ideation update:** Lake Zarovich is now the firm working location, guarded by (or held within) the **Winter Worm** — see [Section 15.2](../archive/s10-prep.md#152--the-winter-worm--the-heart-of-darkness).
  - **Vampire running-water weakness reframed as a plot hook:** since it rarely gives players a real way to exploit it, it's being used to explain why Strahd himself can't retrieve the Heart of Darkness — a legitimate fetch-quest where he offers passage out of Barovia in exchange for retrieving it for him. **2026-09-19 refinement:** [Rahadin](../npcs/rahadin.md), not being a vampire, is Strahd's diver, and Strahd's pitch to the party is framed as a task force to remove the worm "poisoning Vallaki" ([Section 15.5](../archive/s10-prep.md#155--strahds-offer-to-the-party-the-bones-of-saint-andral--the-brides)).
  - **Player-exit mechanic (significant, campaign-shaping):** if the party actually agrees to retrieve the Heart of Darkness and hand it over in exchange for leaving, this will be honored as a real way out. For players who want to leave, that's the end of that character's story (option to roll a new one and keep playing); [Theren](../pcs/theren.md) was specifically named as unlikely to want this. Procedurally: let the table make the choice first, then pull that player aside privately afterward to walk through consequences before finalizing — deliberately not signaling in advance that this is a "safe"/endorsed path. If pursued, **[Madam Eva](../npcs/madam-eva.md) will actively try to prevent it** — she likely doesn't know the Heart is specifically in the lake, but her Fane connection gives her a general premonition of disaster via her cards, and arming Strahd with the Winter Fane's essence puts her in direct opposition to the party's choice.
  - **Card-reading treasure tracker:** [Sun Sword](../items.md#the-sun-sword) → Amber Temple (per Session 9, reachable via an offshoot of the Luna River); the Holy Symbol/[Amulet of Ravenkind](../items.md#the-holy-symbol--amulet-of-ravenkind) → "Five of Swords, the Myrmidon" card, den of wolves overlooking a mountain lake — confirmed in Session 9 as **Luna Lake** specifically; the [Tome of Strahd](tome-of-strahd.md) → generally placed in Vallaki, tied to the "Nine of Glyphs, the Traitor" card: *"I see the ancient foe of an old and noble house. The lost soul will lead you to him."*
