# Vallaki's noble houses — humanizing reworks

**\[IDEA — NONCANONICAL\]** Source: *[Von Richten](../npcs/von-richten.md)'s Darkening Path and Faction Power Struggles*. Both GMs felt the raw-module portrayals of Vallaki's rival houses read as "comically evil" with no clear redeeming trait — see the full NPC entries for [Lady Fiona Watcher](../npcs/lady-fiona-watcher.md), [Stella](../npcs/stella-watcher.md), [Victor Volokovich](../npcs/victor-volokovich.md), [Ernest Larnack](../npcs/ernest-larnack.md), and [Isaac](../npcs/izek-strazani.md) "Izek" Strazani.

  

  - **Lady Fiona Watcher's** redeeming trait: fierce, politically savvy commitment to keeping her declining noble house alive whatever the cost — a Game of Thrones "Tyrell" archetype. Whether she genuinely believes [Strahd](../npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md) is Barovia's rightful ruler is ambiguous; what's certain is she believes her house dies with this generation.
  - **The Volokoviches'** redeeming trait is **still being worked out, not settled**: a self-mythologized identity as Barovia's oldest, truest anti-Strahd house via an ancestral tie to Saint Andrew, undercut by generations of unchallenged safety curdling into complacent self-satisfaction. Concretely: they refuse Barovia's village refugees entry into Vallaki, dismissing them as potential Strahd spies, despite the village having sent aid and refugees of its own to Vallaki — intended to specifically infuriate [Ireena](../npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) at the table. Family catchphrase: "**All will be well**" (Baron [Vargas](../npcs/baron-vargas-volokovich.md) von Volokovich).
  - **New incidental worldbuilding:** the village of Barovia's refugee population lives in a desperate, half-starving camp just outside Vallaki's walls, kept alive mostly by "a few good people like the [Martikovs](../npcs/the-martikov-family.md)," suffering periodic nighttime werewolf attacks, and surviving partly on [Morgantha](../npcs/morgantha.md)'s dream pastries. A *Baldur's Gate 3*-style perception/save check for anyone who eats one was floated as a nice touch.
  - **2026-09-15/19 update:** the Volokoviches' core drive is now articulated as "keep Strahd out; keep everyone seeming happy" (triggered by Strahd's recent reawakening), plus Vargas's desire for the lake's power. The Watcher House's goal is restoring its standing, via Stella's cure or via positioning for Strahd's new era. The refugee camp is now also ravaged by the **withering sickness** (poison + parasites), and bitten refugees are no longer present. Full detail: **[Vallaki's Factions (current working model)](../archive/s10-prep.md#vallakis-factions-current-working-model)**.
