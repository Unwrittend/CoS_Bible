## Where the party is

- **Location:** inside Vallaki's walls, at night under a full moon, after curfew. They got through the gate by bribing (10 gp) and intimidating the guards, who sent them to the **Blue Water Inn**.
- **Party:** [Reyna](pcs/reyna.md), [Lyasra](pcs/lyasra.md), [Cassandra](pcs/cassandra-priam.md), [Theren](pcs/theren.md), [Gaille](pcs/gaille.md), with [Ireena](npcs/ireena-kolyana.md) and three Barovian refugees: [Emeric](npcs/emeric.md), [Magda](npcs/magda.md) and the child [Jen](npcs/jen.md). Lycius is still asleep from the pie. Whether [Kasimir](npcs/kasimir-velikov.md) came in with them wasn't stated.

- **In-world day:** still the night of [Arabelle](npcs/arabelle.md)'s 7th birthday.

## Top open threads (canon)

- **The refugees are infested with parasitic worms.** The dead incubate icy, gel-encased pupae. [Morgantha](npcs/morgantha.md)'s pies are suspect, but [Emeric](npcs/emeric.md) is infected without eating one, and the camp drinks from Lake Zarovich.
- **Something happened at the gate** right after they entered: shouting, a scream, fire, and a lullaby on a grass whistle. A guard had named "Strassney" as the one who forbids opening the gate.
- **[Arabelle](npcs/arabelle.md) is still missing**; the party plans to visit the toymaker. [Theren](pcs/theren.md) carries [Madam Eva](npcs/madam-eva.md)'s letter, and they owe her a doll.
- **[Stella Watcher](npcs/stella-watcher.md)**: the party has her diary and wants to rescue her.
- **[Belinda](items.md#belinda)** felt she was "in the right place" in Vallaki (GM-only explanation below).
- **[Kasimir](npcs/kasimir-velikov.md)'s private talk with [Gaille](pcs/gaille.md)** is still pending.
- **Theren's missing gold:** the party took it (GM-confirmed). He doesn't know who did.
