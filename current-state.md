# Current state (after Session 12)

*Hand-written summary, 2026-11-14, from [Session 12](sessions/s12.md). Update this page whenever a session is logged. The linked files are authoritative; this page is a pointer, not a source.*

## Where the party is

- **Location:** Vallaki, day one after the full-moon night. They spent the night at **St. Andral's Church**, spoke with [Father Lucien](npcs/father-lucien-petrovich.md) in the morning, then went to [Milivoj](npcs/milivoj.md)'s home.
- **Party:** [Reyna](pcs/reyna.md), [Lyasra](pcs/lyasra.md), [Cassandra](pcs/cassandra-priam.md), [Theren](pcs/theren.md), [Gaille](pcs/gaille.md), with [Ireena](npcs/ireena-kolyana.md). [Emeric](npcs/emeric.md), [Magda](npcs/magda.md) and [Gen](npcs/gen.md) came into Vallaki with them. Cassandra's familiar Lycius is now **addicted to the dream pies**.
- **Earlier in Vallaki ([Session 11.5](sessions/s11-5.md)):** [Reyna](pcs/reyna.md) knocked out a guard and broke into the Blue Water Inn, losing an eye to a crossbow-wielding clown ([Rictavio](npcs/von-richten.md), unknown to the party). Cassandra disguised herself as Ireena; the real Ireena hid invisible in the stockyard.

## Top open threads (canon)

- **St. Andral's bones were stolen.** [Yeska](npcs/yeska.md) spoke a prayer aloud that revealed where they were; [Milivoj](npcs/milivoj.md) overheard and took them. The party met and bribed Milivoj, who said **the coffin maker** hired him. They don't know the coffin maker's name. This is the likely cause of the flicker in the hallowing that [Reyna](pcs/reyna.md) sensed in [Session 11.5](sessions/s11-5.md).
- **The Barovian refugee children were taken.** [Cassandra](pcs/cassandra-priam.md)'s divination showed them marching **towards a den overlooking a mountain lake**. The party doesn't know who took them.
- **[Arabelle](npcs/arabelle.md) is still missing**; the party doesn't know where she is. [Theren](pcs/theren.md) carries [Madam Eva](npcs/madam-eva.md)'s letter, and they owe her a doll.
- **Reyna's night of crimes:** she assaulted a guard of the watch and broke into the Blue Water Inn before seeking refuge in front of the whole congregation.
- **The refugees are infested with parasitic worms.** [Morgantha](npcs/morgantha.md)'s pies are suspect, but [Emeric](npcs/emeric.md) is infected without eating one, and the camp drinks from Lake Zarovich.
- **[Stella Watcher](npcs/stella-watcher.md)**: the party has her diary and wants to rescue her.
- **[Belinda](items.md#belinda)** felt she was "in the right place" in Vallaki (GM-only explanation below).
- **[Kasimir](npcs/kasimir-velikov.md)'s private talk with [Gaille](pcs/gaille.md)** is still pending.
- **Theren's missing gold:** the party took it (GM-confirmed). He doesn't know who did.

## GM-only (known to the GMs, not the party)

- The coffin maker is [Henrik van der Voort](npcs/henrik-van-der-voort.md). [Arabelle](npcs/arabelle.md) is trapped in his house (GM-confirmed 2026-10-14): her clairvoyance led her to the vampire spawn nest he is hosting there, and he kidnapped her to keep it secret and is hiding her from the spawn. The bones trail now leads to the same house.
- The refugee children were taken by **werewolves** to the werewolf den.
- The guard [Reyna](pcs/reyna.md) knocked out was [Milivoj](npcs/milivoj.md)'s brother [Dobri](npcs/milivoj.md#dobri). **He is dead, killed by [Von Richten](npcs/von-richten.md)** (GM-confirmed 2026-11-14); the method is deliberately left open.
- [Belinda](items.md#belinda)'s "right place" is her sister [Gertruda](npcs/gertruda-stiegler.md), who is in hiding with [Von Richten](npcs/von-richten.md) as a carnival troupe (GM-confirmed 2026-10-21).
- [Eliza](npcs/eliza.md) is [Strahd](npcs/strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s spy and went to Castle Ravenloft.
- [Ludmilla Vilisevic](npcs/strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic) survived [Session 9](sessions/s09.md): a portal pulled her back to the castle. The party believes she is dead.
- **Still [IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** from the [Session 12 prep](prep/s12-vallaki.md): [Izek](npcs/izek-strazani.md) holding the gate and leaving the refugees to die; Von Richten's alliance with the [Martikovs](npcs/the-martikov-family.md); [Anastrasya](npcs/strahds-brides.md#anastrasya-karelova)'s plan to move the bones out (day 2) before they are gone on day 3, when Strahd attacks the Festival; [Vargas](npcs/baron-vargas-volokovich.md)'s festival parade.
