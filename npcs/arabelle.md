### Arabelle

**Source characterization:** RAW, adapted. Called Madam Eva's granddaughter in play.

**Role:** Luvash's seven-year-old daughter

**Current narrative status (canonical):** **Missing in Vallaki** since about 9 a.m. on her 7th birthday (Session 10), lost in the marketplace on the way to Blinsky's toy shop for a birthday doll. Madam Eva had asked the party to bring her a birthday letter and money (Theren carries the letter) and to buy her a doll. Who took her, and where she is now, is open (Section 15.8).
