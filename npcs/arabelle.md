### Arabelle

**Source characterization:** RAW, adapted. Called [Madam Eva](madam-eva.md)'s granddaughter in play.

**Role:** [Luvash](luvash.md)'s seven-year-old daughter

**Key characterization principles:**

  - **Has clairvoyance (GM-confirmed 2026-10-14).** Not yet shown or revealed at the table.

**Current narrative status (canonical):** **Missing in Vallaki** since about 9 a.m. on her 7th birthday ([Session 10](../sessions/s10.md)), lost in the marketplace on the way to Blinsky's toy shop for a birthday doll. Madam Eva had asked the party to bring her a birthday letter and money ([Theren](../pcs/theren.md) carries the letter) and to buy her a doll. Who took her, and where she is now, was open ([Section 15.8](../archive/s10-prep.md#158--arabelle-undecided)) until the GM confirmation below.

**GM-confirmed canon (2026-10-14), unknown to the party:** Arabelle is **trapped in the coffin maker's house** ([Henrik van der Voort](henrik-van-der-voort.md)). Her clairvoyance drew her to the vampire spawn nest. Henrik kidnapped her so she couldn't tell anyone he is hosting vampire spawn. He doesn't want her to die, so he is hiding her and hoping the spawn don't notice her. *(What changed: this replaces the Section 15.8 options — Lake Zarovich via an infected host or druid, [Von Richten](von-richten.md)'s wagon — none of which were canon. It also means her disappearance isn't connected to [Eliza](eliza.md)'s, unless the GMs later decide otherwise.)*
