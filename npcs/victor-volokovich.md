### Victor Volokovich

**Source characterization:** RAW;

  

**Role:** The Baron's son; possesses a dangerously botched teleportation circle; a possible lead to the Tome of Strahd

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - In possession of a dangerously botched teleportation circle (per raw material).
  - Family trait: vanity, including staring into ornate mirrors — this alone is meant to organically point players toward the enchanted Spirit Mirror in the Volokovich mansion (see Section 11), without the mirror itself being an active manipulator.
  - **Explicitly rejected:** making the Spirit Mirror an overtly sentient, "basically Tom Riddle" voice whispering to Victor — vetoed as too on-the-nose.
  - Married to Stella Watcher in a Watcher-family political maneuver; was cruel to her, which broke her sanity.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-19:** Victor desperately wants to escape Barovia and hates his father. When he looks into the Spirit Mirror (now a mirror of the future) he sees himself fat and aged into the Burgomaster — his father's image — which feeds his cruelty. Stella's diary notes Victor "is not as nice as he seems."

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play.
