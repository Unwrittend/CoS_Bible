### Mad Mary

**Source characterization:** Dragnacarta's reimagining; grounded psychological instability

  

**Unique Homebrew:**

  

  - Belinda speaks to her

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - She does **not** actually *know* things in a magical sense — she *feels* them in a broken human sense
  - Her dialogue should feel like it's coming from someone who has been awake too long and crying too much
  - Lucid moments are more disturbing than fully unhinged ones — a flash of clarity about her own condition is devastating
  - She loves her daughter in a way that has curdled from protection into something that smothers
