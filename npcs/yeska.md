### Yeska

**Source characterization:** RAW (altar boy of St. Andral's Church)

**Role:** Altar boy to [Father Lucien](father-lucien-petrovich.md) at St. Andral's Church, Vallaki. Shares Lucien's motives; not a separate faction ([Session 11 prep](../prep/s11-vallaki.md)).

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Kind, nervous and devout. Welcomed the party in the Morninglord's name ("the Morninglord welcomes all who seek sanctuary beneath his aegis, especially on the night of a full moon"), but was frightened by the blood on [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md)'s armour ("Miss Knight").
  - Wears the Morninglord's sun symbol, like Lucien.

**Current narrative status (canonical):** Met in [Session 11.5](../sessions/s11-5.md). Served wine to the congregation, took [Gen](gen.md) to sleep with the other children in the centre pews ("closest to the saint"), seated [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md) near the front, and promised to wake the party when Father Lucien finishes praying in the morning.
