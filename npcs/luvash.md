### Luvash

**Source characterization:** RAW (Vistani leader at the Vallaki camp), introduced in [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md)

**Role:** Leader of the Vistani of Vallaki; [Arabelle](arabelle.md)'s father

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Volatile and theatrical. Was whipping [Alexei](alexei.md) for losing his daughter when the party arrived, then switched in an instant to effusive host.
  - "I disagree with what you say, but what matters most is getting my daughter back."
  - Keeps an entry arrangement with Burgomaster [Vargas](baron-vargas-volokovich.md).

**Current narrative status (canonical):** Met in Session 10. His daughter Arabelle has been missing in Vallaki since 9 a.m. on her 7th birthday.

**[Session 11](../sessions/s11.md) (canonical):** Hosted the party at dinner. Explained the camp's full-moon tradition: "sleepovers," with every child roped at the waist to the tent's center and the able-bodied keeping watch. "You never know what will come in the night."
