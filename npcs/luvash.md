### Luvash

**Source characterization:** RAW (Vistani leader at the Vallaki camp), introduced in Session 10

**Role:** Leader of the Vistani of Vallaki; Arabelle's father

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Volatile and theatrical. Was whipping Alexei for losing his daughter when the party arrived, then switched in an instant to effusive host.
  - "I disagree with what you say, but what matters most is getting my daughter back."
  - Keeps an entry arrangement with Burgomaster Vargas (Section 4, Vargas).

**Current narrative status (canonical):** Met in Session 10. His daughter Arabelle has been missing in Vallaki since 9 a.m. on her 7th birthday.
