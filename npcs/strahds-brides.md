### Strahd's Brides — Ludmilla Vilisevic, Anastrasya Karelova, Volenta Popofsky (GM ideation; Ludmilla partly canonical via Session 9)

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** Source: the fan-made guide *"The Brides, Escher, and [Rahadin](rahadin.md)"* (r/CurseofStrahd community homebrew; the guide's own author notes it is "next to none... actual canon lore" and that in RAW Curse of [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md) the brides have no fleshed-out characterization and are simply locked in the crypts). Used here purely as inspiration and adapted/trimmed for this campaign. [Anastrasya](#anastrasya-karelova) and [Volenta](#volenta-popofsky) have not been introduced to the party; [Ludmilla](#ludmilla-vilisevic) has ([Session 9](../sessions/s09.md), unnamed — see below).

**Naming RESOLVED (canonical, confirmed 2026-09-24):** the vampire the party fought in Session 9 — filed below as "The [Woodcutter's Bride](woodcutters-bride.md)" — **was Ludmilla Vilisevic**. She was **not destroyed**: on dropping to 0 HP, an **emergency dimensional portal** pulled her back to Castle Ravenloft. The "sky splitting open" / "nothing remains" the party saw was that portal. *(What changed: the earlier open flag asked whether the two Ludmillas were the same person or separate; they are the same. Her name has still not been spoken to the party, and the party believes they killed her.)* Her fan-guide profile below remains ideation, except where it overlaps with what happened in play.

**2026-09-19 ideation [IDEA — NONCANONICAL]:** Ludmilla is central to Strahd's plan — she has run the long-term poisoning of the Winter Worm since [Lugdana](lugdana.md)'s era with [Baba Lysaga](baba-lysaga.md)'s help, and she is part of his inner trio with Rahadin. Her Session 9 lumber operation now reads as part of that work ([Strahd's Machination: Poisoning the Winter Worm](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-machination-poisoning-the-winter-worm)). "Anastrasia" in transcripts = Anastrasya Karelova, who pursues the Saint Andral's bones heist on her own initiative and doesn't know the grand plan. **Volenta Popofsky** (transcribed "Violetta"; confirmed to be the same character) is "the berserker," placement TBD. Escher attaches himself to Volenta, hoping to manipulate her. See [Strahd's Offer to the Party, the Bones of Saint Andral & the Brides](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-offer-to-the-party-the-bones-of-saint-andral--the-brides).

#### Ludmilla Vilisevic

**Source characterization:** Homebrew, adapted from the fan guide above.

**Role:** Eldest and most senior of [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s three brides — 330 years old, 302 of them as one of Strahd's consorts. An Aristocrat, Puppeteer, Diplomat, and Sorcerer; functions as de facto head of Strahd's harem, though the other brides respect rather than obey her.

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Cold, analytical, and scientific rather than passionate. Genuinely absorbed only by arcane study; performs interest in court gossip and beauty only because it's expected of her.
  - Strahd has given her partial Amber Temple knowledge but forbidden her from investigating it further herself — she still quietly wants to.
  - Goals: keep Strahd's favor permanently (her deepest fear is being set aside and eventually locked away in the crypts once she's no longer useful); track down Barovia's scattered Fey/Amber gems before Strahd learns she's fallen behind; stay the most useful/knowledgeable consort to keep the others from undercutting her; and, longer-term, cultivate a suitable "next bride" so she can eventually step back from active duty without being discarded.
  - Publicly poses in Vallaki as a noblewoman under a cover identity, recruiting adventuring parties toward goals that serve her (investigating werewolves, exposing a spy Burgomaster, dealing with [Izek](izek-strazani.md)'s cruelty) without revealing that she — or any of the brides — are vampires. Keeps her own and the other brides' identities hidden from any party she's using, up until Strahd's court formally "unveils" them.
  - Relationships: genuinely values [Rahadin](rahadin.md) as her closest confidant in the court (unlike her performative regard for almost everyone else) and would hunt down anyone responsible if he were harmed; personally recruited and now manages [Anastrasya](#anastrasya-karelova), treating her as a useful but replaceable underling; sees [Volenta](#volenta-popofsky) as a dangerously immature child she keeps on a short leash; has open contempt for Escher, Strahd's newest and least-tested consort.

**Appearance in play (canonical, [Session 9](../sessions/s09.md)):** a wealthy, dark-skinned noblewoman with parasol, lace, and fine robes. She directed a castle-scale undead lumber operation with at least one wizard ally, fought airborne over the Tser Falls canyon, and used a choking/suffocation mist attack. See the [Woodcutter's Bride](woodcutters-bride.md) entry below for full play details.

**Current narrative status (canonical):** Encountered in Session 9, **unnamed to the party**. Reduced to 0 HP and escaped via an emergency dimensional portal to Castle Ravenloft — **alive**. The party believes they destroyed her. How badly she was hurt, and whether she knows who the party are and has reported them to Strahd, is open ([Open questions](../open-questions.md)).

#### Anastrasya Karelova

**Source characterization:** Homebrew, adapted from the fan guide above.

**Role:** [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s second-eldest bride — 219 years old, 194 as a vampire. A Noble, Artist, Hostess, and Reckless.

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Former Vallakian noblewoman of exceptional, self-regarding beauty; ruthless toward anyone she considered beneath her station even before being turned.
  - Backstory hook: [Ludmilla](#ludmilla-vilisevic) personally recruited her — under the guise of a tour of Castle Ravenloft meant to terrify her into staying loyal to Strahd's court — but [Anastrasya](#anastrasya-karelova) was enthralled by the display of power instead of frightened. Strahd turned her once his interest in Ludmilla began to wane.
  - Bloodthirsty and showy: hosts frequent, lavish parties in Strahd's honor and hunts more often and more loudly than the other brides.
  - Resents Ludmilla as her only real competition for Strahd's attention, but isn't clever enough to act on it effectively; regards [Volenta](#volenta-popofsky)'s presence as a passing annoyance rather than a lasting threat.
  - Goals: keep Strahd's favor forever; organizes/is responsible for a "Feast of St. Andral"-style event tied to Strahd's court calendar; is deeply jealous of the hold the memory of Tatyana — and by extension her present-day reincarnation — has over Strahd, and regards that reincarnation as a serious threat to be removed or permanently confined given the chance.
  - **Cross-reference:** this jealousy hook lines up directly with this bible's existing Tatiana/[Ireena](ireena-kolyana.md) reincarnation lineage ([Tatiana / Ireena reincarnation lineage](../lore/cosmology.md#tatiana--ireena-reincarnation-lineage), where Ireena Kolyana is established as Tatiana's most recent reincarnation) — worth keeping in mind if any of the three brides ever get seeded as a threat to Ireena specifically.

  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation ([Session 11 prep](../prep/s11-vallaki.md)):** A major faction in the Vallaki timeline, separate from Ludmilla. **If her bones scheme succeeds, it triggers the Feast of St. Andral** (Strahd's forces attack Vallaki — [Major events in the three-day window](../prep/s11-vallaki.md#major-events-in-the-three-day-window)). She's played as hot-headed rather than calculating, partly to tell the brides apart. *Considered and dropped:* having her send a raid or assassination attempt at Ireena (the brides know harming Ireena would anger Strahd, and it read as too calculated for her).

**Current narrative status:** [IDEA — NONCANONICAL] Not yet introduced to the party.

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-10-27 ideation ([Session 12 prep](../prep/s12-vallaki.md)):** Anastrasya was present to see [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md)'s attack at the Blue Water Inn. After [Milivoj](milivoj.md) steals the bones on night one, she plans how to get them out of the city (she can't touch them herself) and sets traps in [Henrik](henrik-van-der-voort.md)'s house: vampire spawn and zombies, and possibly a teleportation circle for the bones. The hallowing fades with distance; she needs a day or two. Day 2 the bones leave the church, day 3 they're gone and Strahd attacks during the Festival.

#### Volenta Popofsky

**Source characterization:** Homebrew, adapted from the fan guide above.

**Role:** Youngest and newest-turned of the three brides — 113 years old, 95 of them as one of [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s consorts. A Socialite and a Sadist.

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Former Barovian sex worker who would torture and kill customers who crossed her; fled to Castle Ravenloft when this was discovered and offered herself to Strahd, who was impressed by her cruelty and turned her at 18.
  - Sociopathic and genuinely childlike/petulant in most non-violent interactions — has no real empathy for anyone, believes (against explicit warnings from both [Ludmilla](#ludmilla-vilisevic) and [Rahadin](rahadin.md)) that Strahd's love for her is unconditional and permanent.
  - The most willful and chaotic of the three brides; enjoys testing boundaries with Ludmilla and Rahadin specifically, though she knows better than to actually cross Rahadin.
  - Collects trophies from her kills; carries/wears a skull taken from a werewolf she killed as proof of loyalty to Strahd, which — per the fan source — has absorbed a bloodlust curse from years of use. **Optional item hook, not yet adopted:** if Chris wants to bring this into the campaign, it would need its own [Key Items](../items.md) entry (a cursed mask/trophy item); flagging here rather than adding that entry unprompted.
  - Goals: sees Strahd's favor as constantly at risk despite reassurance; regards [Ireena Kolyana](ireena-kolyana.md) (if the party's Ireena ever becomes known to the brides) as her single biggest threat and would engineer her death or ruin given the chance; wants to see Barovia's winery destroyed largely for the chaos and disruption of it, not for any strategic reason; enjoys sabotaging [Anastrasya](#anastrasya-karelova) for her own amusement.
  - Prefers psychological torment and games over direct combat; would rather stalk, frighten, and toy with a target than fight fairly.

**Current narrative status:** [IDEA — NONCANONICAL] Not yet introduced to the party.
