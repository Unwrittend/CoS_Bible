### Violet Larkwing

**Source characterization:** Homebrew

  

**Role:** [Theren](../pcs/theren.md)'s mother; village druid

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - Fiercely protective of her children; will name foul magic before accepting a mundane explanation
  - Emotionally volatile under grief but not fragile — her anger is directed outward, not inward
  - Likely a useful ally and source of information for Theren
