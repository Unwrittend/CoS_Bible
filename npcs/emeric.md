### Emeric

**Source characterization:** [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) prep doc ([Vallaki Gates — the Refugee Camp](../archive/s10-prep.md#b-vallaki-gates--the-refugee-camp)); Barovian refugee

**Spelling:** "Emeric" per the GMs' prep doc; the [Session 11](../sessions/s11.md) transcript renders it "Emmerich."

**Role:** Barovian refugee outside Vallaki; one of [Ireena](ireena-kolyana.md)'s people

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Melancholy older man with grey hair. Said the pies make their worries go away and bring the most pleasant dreams, "and some of us just want to go back to that dream."

**Current narrative status (canonical):** Met by [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md) at the refugee camp in [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) (off-screen) and again in [Session 11](../sessions/s11.md). Infected with the parasites (white-blue lines with something moving beneath) **though he says he hasn't eaten a pie**. Reyna's Lay on Hands stopped the movement; the marks remain. Brought the party two corpses for [Theren](../pcs/theren.md)'s autopsy, and went into Vallaki with the party. **[Session 11.5](../sessions/s11-5.md):** hid in the stockyard with Ireena; restless about the fire beyond the gate.
