### Von Richten (Rudolph van Richten)

**Status: \[IDEA — NONCANONICAL major rework of a published-module character.\]** His [Session 2](../sessions/s02.md) rescue of [Gertruda](gertruda-stiegler.md) is already canonical; everything below expands on his motivations and methods and has not been revealed to players.

  

**Roleplaying Information**

  

***Resonance.*** A once-noble figure whose long grief and vengeance have curdled him into something darker — sympathetic in his backstory, unsettling in his present methods.

  

***Emotions.*** Jaded, controlled, increasingly cold; capable of real tenderness (toward Gertruda, historically toward [Esmeralda](esmeralda-davenir.md)) that coexists uneasily with his cruelty toward the Vistani.

  

***Motivations.*** Vengeance against the Vistani broadly (not just the family who sold his son Erasmus) — since Erasmus was sold to [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s court and killed. Recently developed a new fixation: experimenting on [Ireena](ireena-kolyana.md) the moment he learns she can resist vampirism (see [The three-way power struggle: Dark Powers vs. Strahd vs. the Fanes](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#the-three-way-power-struggle-dark-powers-vs-strahd-vs-the-fanes)), framed as another symptom of his darkening arc.

  

**Character Information**

  

**Aliases**: Rictavio; Alenik, a disguise Von Richten uses (GM-confirmed 2026-10-10; spelled "Alanik" in the [Session 5](../sessions/s05.md) log until standardized to Alenik on 2026-10-10)

  

***Persona.*** No longer just a grim vampire hunter — reconceived as someone who corrals/traps weaker undead (echoing *The Walking Dead*'s caged-walker encampments) and maintains multiple hidden undead depots across Barovia, tied directly into the existing "flesh map" cache system: some caches the party finds are *his*, not Strahd's. Concealment method: pitfall-trap-style pits, which doubles as an explanation for some of Barovia's missing villagers (fell in, turned, and either escaped or wandered home — feeding some of the undead that hit the [Session 4](../sessions/s04.md) siege).

  

***Morale.*** Cannot rest peacefully near the undead he attracts, so he's drawn to consecrated/holy nexuses — part of why Vallaki (protected via consecration) appeals to him, and why he's shown recent interest in St. Andrew's Church there specifically.

  

***Relationships.*** Does **not** yet know he is being drawn toward the Dark Powers' attention — Michelle's clarifying note: the Dark Powers don't control him yet, they find him increasingly *enticing* as a potential asset given his own moral decay. He's a third-party actor drawing unwanted attention, not yet compromised.

  

**Confirmed items/details:**

  

  - **[Ring of Mind Shielding](../items.md#ring-of-mind-shielding-von-richten)** (see [Key Items](../items.md)) — immune to thought-reading, alignment-detection, and creature-type detection; anyone attempting to read his mind finds it "empty."
  - An unnamed homebrew magic bedroll/sleeping bag (see [Key Items](../items.md)) that causes anyone inside it to register as undead/aberration to detection magic — still needs full mechanical stat-out and an in-fiction concealment method (a bag of holding or similar), since a "corpse-smelling sleeping bag" would otherwise draw immediate suspicion.
  - **Timeline:** in Barovia roughly 15–20 years (revised down from an initial 25–30 estimate to keep his age plausible as a young doctor when his son was taken). Was once a doctor who spent his time and money healing others — a kindness now curdled into vengeance.
  - **Hobbies:** wine connoisseurship offers a fetch-quest hook — he could offer passage out of Barovia in exchange for bottles from the **Wizard of Wines** vineyard, run by the [Martikov](the-martikov-family.md) family. (Cross-reference: this idea actually belongs to Strahd's characterization, not Von Richten's — see [Full profile notes cross-reference](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#full-profile-notes-cross-reference). Included here as a reminder these two NPCs' arcs may intersect at the Martikov vineyard.)
  - **First appearance confirmed:** he will **not** appear human-looking to the party on first encounter — reads as a vampire through and through.
  - **GM-confirmed canon (2026-10-21):** Von Richten and [Gertruda](gertruda-stiegler.md) are **in hiding together as a carnival troupe** (consistent with his RAW Rictavio persona). [Belinda](../items.md#belinda)'s "right place" pull in Vallaki ([Session 11](../sessions/s11.md)) points to Gertruda. Not yet known to the party.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation ([Session 11 prep](../prep/s11-vallaki.md)):** **Zombie swarm on the Vistani.** He's been collecting zombies in his pits to strike at Strahd and anything he sees as Strahd-aligned — chiefly the Vistani. Unless he's dissuaded, he brings a zombie horde against the Vistani camp outside Vallaki during the three-day window ([Major events in the three-day window](../prep/s11-vallaki.md#major-events-in-the-three-day-window)). His tiger was mentioned as an alternative, but the zombies tie better into what's already set up.
  - **Discarded thread:** an earlier idea that the child's body recovered in [Session 0.5b](../sessions/s00-5b.md)/[Session 1](../sessions/s01.md) (Erasmus) was a vampire spawn was walked back — the internal logic didn't hold (a vampire spawn wouldn't have a heartbeat, and [Dismas](dismas.md)'s exam found one). Benched rather than force-retconned; pieces of it could be salvaged later if useful.

  

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-10-27 ideation ([Session 12 prep](../prep/s12-vallaki.md)):** On the night of Session 11, as Rictavio, he shot [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md), defended the Blue Water Inn and killed a guard, [Dobry](milivoj.md#dobry), in front of the inn. He hid the body in his wagon in the stockyard and fed it to his saber-toothed tiger (whether [Gertruda](gertruda-stiegler.md) helped is open). Urwin Martikov confronts him ("you're no travelling circus leader"), and they strike an uneasy alliance: he wants a local front, the Martikovs want the druids' whereabouts, and they disapprove of his brutality. On day one he's in hiding with Gertruda, not at the inn. See [The Martikovs and Von Richten](../prep/s12-vallaki.md#the-martikovs-and-von-richten).
