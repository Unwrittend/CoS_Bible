### Von Richten (Rudolph van Richten)

**Status: \[IDEA — NONCANONICAL major rework of a published-module character.\]** His Session 2 rescue of Gertruda is already canonical; everything below expands on his motivations and methods and has not been revealed to players.

  

**Roleplaying Information**

  

***Resonance.*** A once-noble figure whose long grief and vengeance have curdled him into something darker — sympathetic in his backstory, unsettling in his present methods.

  

***Emotions.*** Jaded, controlled, increasingly cold; capable of real tenderness (toward Gertruda, historically toward Esmeralda) that coexists uneasily with his cruelty toward the Vistani.

  

***Motivations.*** Vengeance against the Vistani broadly (not just the family who sold his son Erasmus) — since Erasmus was sold to Strahd's court and killed. Recently developed a new fixation: experimenting on Ireena the moment he learns she can resist vampirism (see Section 9 §11), framed as another symptom of his darkening arc.

  

**Character Information**

  

**Aliases**: Rictavio; Alenik

  

***Persona.*** No longer just a grim vampire hunter — reconceived as someone who corrals/traps weaker undead (echoing *The Walking Dead*'s caged-walker encampments) and maintains multiple hidden undead depots across Barovia, tied directly into the existing "flesh map" cache system: some caches the party finds are *his*, not Strahd's. Concealment method: pitfall-trap-style pits, which doubles as an explanation for some of Barovia's missing villagers (fell in, turned, and either escaped or wandered home — feeding some of the undead that hit the Session 4 siege).

  

***Morale.*** Cannot rest peacefully near the undead he attracts, so he's drawn to consecrated/holy nexuses — part of why Vallaki (protected via consecration) appeals to him, and why he's shown recent interest in St. Andrew's Church there specifically.

  

***Relationships.*** Does **not** yet know he is being drawn toward the Dark Powers' attention — Michelle's clarifying note: the Dark Powers don't control him yet, they find him increasingly *enticing* as a potential asset given his own moral decay. He's a third-party actor drawing unwanted attention, not yet compromised.

  

**Confirmed items/details:**

  

  - **Ring of Mind Shielding** (see Section 5) — immune to thought-reading, alignment-detection, and creature-type detection; anyone attempting to read his mind finds it "empty."
  - An unnamed homebrew magic bedroll/sleeping bag (see Section 5) that causes anyone inside it to register as undead/aberration to detection magic — still needs full mechanical stat-out and an in-fiction concealment method (a bag of holding or similar), since a "corpse-smelling sleeping bag" would otherwise draw immediate suspicion.
  - **Timeline:** in Barovia roughly 15–20 years (revised down from an initial 25–30 estimate to keep his age plausible as a young doctor when his son was taken). Was once a doctor who spent his time and money healing others — a kindness now curdled into vengeance.
  - **Hobbies:** wine connoisseurship offers a fetch-quest hook — he could offer passage out of Barovia in exchange for bottles from the **Wizard of Wines** vineyard, run by the Mardikov family. (Cross-reference: this idea actually belongs to Strahd's characterization, not Von Richten's — see Section 9 §10.2. Included here as a reminder these two NPCs' arcs may intersect at the Mardikov vineyard.)
  - **First appearance confirmed:** he will **not** appear human-looking to the party on first encounter — reads as a vampire through and through.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation (Section 16):** **Zombie swarm on the Vistani.** He's been collecting zombies in his pits to strike at Strahd and anything he sees as Strahd-aligned — chiefly the Vistani. Unless he's dissuaded, he brings a zombie horde against the Vistani camp outside Vallaki during the three-day window (Section 16.2). His tiger was mentioned as an alternative, but the zombies tie better into what's already set up.
  - **Discarded thread:** an earlier idea that the child's body recovered in Session 0.5b/Session 1 (Erasmus) was a vampire spawn was walked back — the internal logic didn't hold (a vampire spawn wouldn't have a heartbeat, and Dismas's exam found one). Benched rather than force-retconned; pieces of it could be salvaged later if useful.
