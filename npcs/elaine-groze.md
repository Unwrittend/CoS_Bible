### Elaine Groze

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** Source: 2026-09-19 ideation ([Section 15](../archive/s10-prep.md).7A).

**Source characterization:** New homebrew

**Role:** [Morgantha](morgantha.md)'s "granddaughter" and helper

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Named from Arthurian legend (Elaine, alongside Morgan le Fay → Morgantha). "[Violet](violet-larkwing.md)" was rejected as too close to [Volenta](strahds-brides.md#volenta-popofsky) (transcribed "Violetta"); "Beth" and "Anna" (Annabelle clash) were also considered.
  - Presented as Morgantha's granddaughter. Whether she's actually blood-related is unsettled — Michelle first floated her as Bella's child, then leaned toward her being an unrelated Barovian girl. **Surname: Groze** (from the [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) Prep doc, which spells her first name "Elllaine" — confirm Elaine vs. Ellaine). The prep doc's Divine Sense read-aloud describes her as an unsullied mortal child with darkness licking at her — so **not** hag-kin.
  - Very callous and quite cruel: reads [Stella Watcher](stella-watcher.md)'s diary aloud mockingly, over the top, laughing with Morgantha.

**Current narrative status:** **Encountered in Session 10 (canonical)**, essentially as written in 15.10A: theatrically read Stella's diary aloud and laughed with Morgantha, shook the book until pages fell out, then traded it to [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md) for four pieces of candy. Frightened by Reyna's stare, she hid in Morgantha's robes. Morgantha called her "Elaine" once; the transcript otherwise renders it "Elena" — spelling still to confirm.
