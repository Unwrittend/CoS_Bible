### Reysatra

  

**Source Characterization:** Homebrew  
**Race/Class:** Dragonborn Paladin

  

**Character Background:**  
Was called by Argonvaust to protect the Amber Temple from evil. Reysatra volentarily entered Barovia at Argonvaust's call and joined the Order of the Silver Dragon. She died alongside the order. She is now a revenant, and her soul is stuck in Barovia.

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - Does not understand common well. Fluent in Elvish
  - Awkward but immensely loyal
