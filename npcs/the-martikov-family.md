### The Martikov Family

**Source characterization:** RAW

  

**Role:** A family lineage of hereditary archivists preserving the true history of Strahd's death/binding cycles; run the Wizard of Wines vineyard

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - The last surviving lineage with direct, deliberate historical knowledge of the full Strahd death-and-return cycle — effectively hereditary bookkeepers/archivists, distinct from the villagers' incomplete "hero worship" version of events. Kolyan Indirovich may have left hidden records with them.
  - Explicitly meant to be a discoverable thread for the party, not something disclosed directly.
  - Run the **Wizard of Wines** vineyard — tied to Strahd's wine-connoisseur fetch-quest hook (see Section 9 §10.2) and to a possible false pretext for Strahd to move against them (accusing the vineyard of failing, or the family of being "possessed by vampiric ravens").
  - One of only two fully unconquered "anti-Strahd" bastions left in Barovia in the present-day faction map, alongside Kresk (see Section 10).
  - Identified as loosely "tied to" the Fanes faction in the three-way power struggle (see Section 9 §11), though their exact role — allies, agents, or something else — is unspecified.

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet directly encountered in play; referenced via Kolyan and the historical timeline.

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation (Section 16):** Named members, per RAW: **Urwin Martikov** (patriarch; runs the Blue Water Inn), his wife **Danika**, and Urwin's father **Davian**. Davian is listed separately because his motive differs from the rest of the family's; the others share one faction motive.
