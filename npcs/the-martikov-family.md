### The Martikov Family

**Spelling (GM-confirmed 2026-10-10):** earlier ideation notes ([Cosmology](../lore/cosmology.md), [Lugdana](lugdana.md), [Ismark the Great](ismark-the-great.md)) called this family "Mordakov" or "Mardikov"; standardized to Martikov throughout on 2026-10-10. A source transcript title in the [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) prep archive keeps "Mardikovs" as written.

**Source characterization:** RAW

  

**Role:** A family lineage of hereditary archivists preserving the true history of [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s death/binding cycles; run the Wizard of Wines vineyard

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - The last surviving lineage with direct, deliberate historical knowledge of the full Strahd death-and-return cycle — effectively hereditary bookkeepers/archivists, distinct from the villagers' incomplete "hero worship" version of events. Kolyan Indirovich may have left hidden records with them.
  - Explicitly meant to be a discoverable thread for the party, not something disclosed directly.
  - Run the **Wizard of Wines** vineyard — tied to Strahd's wine-connoisseur fetch-quest hook (see [Full profile notes cross-reference](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#full-profile-notes-cross-reference)) and to a possible false pretext for Strahd to move against them (accusing the vineyard of failing, or the family of being "possessed by vampiric ravens").
  - One of only two fully unconquered "anti-Strahd" bastions left in Barovia in the present-day faction map, alongside Kresk (see [Strahd's motivations](../lore/strahd-motivations.md)).
  - Identified as loosely "tied to" the Fanes faction in the three-way power struggle (see [The three-way power struggle: Dark Powers vs. Strahd vs. the Fanes](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#the-three-way-power-struggle-dark-powers-vs-strahd-vs-the-fanes)), though their exact role — allies, agents, or something else — is unspecified.

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet directly encountered in play; referenced via Kolyan and the historical timeline.

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation ([Session 11 prep](../prep/s11-vallaki.md)):** Named members, per RAW: **Urwin Martikov** (patriarch; runs the Blue Water Inn), his wife **Danika**, and Urwin's father **Davian**. Davian is listed separately because his motive differs from the rest of the family's; the others share one faction motive.

  

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-10-27 ideation ([Session 12 prep](../prep/s12-vallaki.md)):** The Martikovs (with crows calling overhead) watched Rictavio defend the Blue Water Inn from the party and kill a guard. **Urwin** confronts him that night, and they form an uneasy alliance. Through [Lugdana](lugdana.md)'s history (her party allied the Indirovich line, vampire hunters and the Martikovs against Strahd) they may already have known of [Von Richten](von-richten.md). They want **the whereabouts of the druids**, since they know of Lugdana's quest to reconnect with the Fanes. They prefer to stay unseen and disapprove of his killing [Dobry](milivoj.md#dobry).
