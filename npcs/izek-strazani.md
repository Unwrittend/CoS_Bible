### Isaac "Izek" Strazani

**Source characterization:** RAW

  

**Role:** The Baron's mutated-armed personal enforcer

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - **CANONICAL (GM-confirmed 2026-09-24): Izek is Ireena's brother.** Not yet revealed at the table. Per the 09-19 ideation, **Izek does not know they are related.**
  - Functions as part of the Volokovich mansion's defensive complication, alongside roughly 36 retained mansion guards (per raw material), for any heist-style approach to the Tome of Strahd questline (see Section 11).
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] Backstory (2026-09-19 version, superseding the 2026-09-15 draft):** an orphan on Vallaki's streets who went fishing on Lake Zarovich out of hunger. One of the Winter Worm's whirlpools pulled him down, and he touched the worm (earlier draft: touched the Heart of Darkness itself) and was infected. Because the worm slept, his parasite lay **dormant for years** and only became active recently — which is why the arm now looks grotesque. Vargas found or rescued him, and this bond is the root of Izek's devotion. As a child he kept insisting there was a monster in the lake, and only Vargas believed him. He is indebted to Vargas but never calls him "father," which would imply a claim on Victor's title. He refers to him by title ("the Burgomaster").
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] Ireena fixation (2026-09-19):** Izek has always dreamed of red-haired women — a shameful fixation he hides, since it reads as such a "Strahd move" and Vargas is fiercely anti-Strahd. He believes the woman in his dreams is the one he's meant to be with. Per RAW, he has the Dollmaker make dolls resembling her. **His interaction with Ireena hinges entirely on her hair:** with it dyed black, he won't identify her at first, and Chris does not want him to at the start. It could surface days later in-game if the dye grows out or washes off, or if he catches her unguarded. If he realizes, he tries to **abduct** her. Likely fight locations: the Volokovich mansion (a hush-hush prisoner basement), or a cloak-and-dagger night attack in which he hides his identity and blasts from range with his arm (eldritch-blast-style, cold/necrotic/void-themed). Ireena is unlikely to be sleeping alone, so any attempt probably sparks a fight.
  - **Sibling tie:** the relationship itself is now **canon** (2026-09-24). The *details* of how they were separated remain **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]**: Chris's 09-19 version has Ireena found in the Svalich Woods by Kolyan while Izek reached Vallaki as an orphan, and Michelle raised timeline concerns about it. The whirlpool/dormant-parasite backstory above was built to work either way.

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play. **Note:** the canonical sibling connection to Ireena has significant potential for the Vallaki arc — consider how and when to seed it. His red-hair fixation and possible abduction attempt read very differently once the relationship is known.

**Session 10 (canonical, by reputation only):** Morgantha warned the party about "the Burgomaster's attack dog, Izek": recognizable by a hideous, mangled arm, a spellcaster of some sort, and he has **set Barovians who opposed the Burgomaster on fire**. Reyna heard at the refugee camp that people are being burned and robbed. His **fire** magic is therefore canon; the cold/necrotic blasts for a disguised night attack remain ideation.

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation (Section 16):** On most of the party's infiltration routes, **Izek walks in on the guards or the section they're using.** The party makes a group Perception check against a combined total (roughly 30–40, TBD); success means they hear his heavy footsteps coming, failure means he surprises them ("State your business"). Combat is always possible, but explore non-combat options first. He would throw intruders in the **standard town prison** (not the mansion's private one) rather than bring them to Vargas — unless he thinks they'd help Vargas's plan. If they enter at night, they may see him **leaving Blinsky's toy shop with a satchel** (collecting his dolls). During a werewolf raid on the refugee camp, he gathers guards to hold the gate and strikes only wolves that come too close; otherwise he lets the Barovians die.
