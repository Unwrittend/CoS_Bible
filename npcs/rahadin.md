### Rahadin

**Source characterization:** RAW module, Dragnacarta, expanded

  

**Role:** [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s Chamberlain and primary agent

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - Confirmed as canon within the ideation sessions (though still unconfirmed at the table): has sacrificed the Dusk Elves for some purpose — whether for the Dark Powers or his own ends is undetermined.
  - **Floated, explicitly NOT canonized even at the GM level:** that Rahadin struck a deal with the Dark Powers to resurrect Strahd each time he dies. Chris likes it as a possible throughline but both GMs agreed to leave it open rather than lock it in.
  - Called the hit on Erasmus (already established elsewhere in this bible under [Dismas](dismas.md)'s key relationships) — unknown to Dismas.
  - Cousin of [Casimir](kasimir-velikov.md) Velikov, leader of the Dusk Elves, and uncle by extension to the late Dusk Elf prince (see Casimir's entry below).
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-19:** Rahadin is part of Strahd's inner trio (with Strahd and [Ludmilla](strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic)) who know the real Lake Zarovich plan. Not being a vampire, he is the one who can **lead the dive to kill the Winter Worm and retrieve the [Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness)**, with undead helpers, the party (if they accept Strahd's offer) or werewolves (if not), and a contingency teleport. Strahd trusting him with this is meant as an on-screen show of Rahadin's loyalty. See Sections 15.4–15.5.

  

**Current narrative status:** Already met by the party — informed Ismark of Kolyan's death and his own ascension to "new Burgomaster" ([Session 5](../sessions/s05.md)).
