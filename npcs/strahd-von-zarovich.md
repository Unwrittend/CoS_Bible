### Strahd von Zarovich

**Status: \[IDEA — NONCANONICAL elaboration on a published-module character.\]** Baseline facts (he is Barovia's vampire lord, killed his brother Sergei, obsesses over Tatiana's reincarnations) are pre-existing module canon; everything below is homebrew psychology/goals work not yet revealed to players.

  

**Roleplaying Information**

  

***Resonance.*** Complicated, not cartoonishly hateable — except in one deliberate place: his pursuit of Tatiana/[Ireena](ireena-kolyana.md), which should land as viscerally "icky"/predatory (a hunter enjoying the chase; "wooing" as part of the hunt), so players want to see him defeated on that front specifically even while finding him sympathetic elsewhere.

  

***Emotions.*** Egocentric, prideful, entitled — masked by a polite, cunning, "gentlemanly" facade that is explicitly a mask, not a genuine trait.

  

***Motivations.*** Two root motivations, from which everything else descends: (1) **mastery of his own fate/destiny** — refusal to be controlled by anyone, chiefly the Dark Powers; and (2) a genuine **love of entertainment/spectacle**. Goals downstream of these: revenge on those who've killed him across past cycles; obtaining more power; escaping Barovia; claiming Ireena/Tatiana (belief that he owns her); building his spy network and military strength.

  

**Character Information**

  

***Persona.*** Does not actually control the mists himself — that's the Dark Powers'/Fanes' domain. The belief that he does is a deliberately maintained illusion, upheld by the Vistani (who serve his interests for freedom to travel) and by killing/compelling anyone who tries to leave alone before word gets out. Only a handful of people know the deeper truth (it's enforced by werewolves, not his personal magic): [Stanimir](stanimir.md) and [Madam Eva](madam-eva.md) certainly; [Arturi](arturi.md) likely.

  

***Morale.*** Values entertainment and spectacle; prefers manipulation, coercion, and long games over blunt force where possible.

  

***Relationships.*** Views virtually everyone in Barovia (barring the Vistani, his "birds") as living in a cage, and believes what he could offer Tatiana/Ireena — wealth, protection, status — is a bigger, more comfortable cage than anyone else in Barovia could give her. Blames Sergei for his own death in his internal accounting ("too bullheaded to see he could never win her back") rather than taking responsibility; views Sergei and Tatiana's romance with a kind of contemptuous dismissal ("high school love"). If he can't escape Barovia, he'd keep Tatiana as a vampire-spawn companion; if he can escape and she still refuses him, he'd leave without her rather than delay his own freedom — but would frame it to himself as teaching her a lesson about her own "childishness," never as self-preservation.

  

**Explicitly rejected directions (do not use):** Strahd secretly being a lich with a hidden phylactery; vampirism as a side-effect of a shard-powered ritual gone right; making "win Tatiana's true love" his sole/primary motivation (felt too flat — "achy"). Preferred alternative to the last one: Strahd may believe (mistakenly or not) that Tatiana/her reincarnations literally **are** the Autumn Fane, since she represents the future and he has always sought his future through her — reframing pursuit of her as instrumental (a possible path out of the mists), not pure romantic obsession.

  

**Personal traits/hobbies:** Falconry, and genuine wine connoisseurship (blood and wine aren't the same sense, so a vampire's dulled palate doesn't preclude this) — part of why he grew fond of Barovia's wild grapes in the first place. Owns a tamed Roc (a demonstration of falconry skill), possibly the corrupted/subjugated version of a Roc originally belonging to Madam Eva (keeping the bird motif tied to the Autumn Fane rather than introducing an unrelated symbol for him — see [Full profile notes cross-reference](../lore/strahd-motivations.md#full-profile-notes-cross-reference) and [Session 9 prep](../archive/s09-prep.md)).

  

**Extensive lore not yet touched on above:** full goals/motivations table, the three-way Dark Powers/Strahd/Fanes power struggle, Ireena's anti-curse blessing and why his usual method failed on her, the wine/[Martikov](the-martikov-family.md) fetch-quest hook, the dinner-invitation and reward/boon mechanic, and the death/resurrection timeline are all detailed in **[Cosmology](../lore/cosmology.md)** — refer there rather than duplicating here.

**[Session 9](../sessions/s09.md) note \[player theory, unconfirmed\]:** the party, discussing the Tarokka "lost soul" clue, floated the theory that the ally described (a "soul unmoored from its body, bound still to duty... a blade that does not know it has already fallen") could be Strahd's slain brother — i.e. that he "didn't actually die" in the way the myth says. This is party speculation prompted by [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md)'s campfire story, not a GM-confirmed fact — flagging here so it isn't mistaken for established lore.

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-19 — Strahd's active scheme:** his current concrete machination is obtaining the **[Heart of Darkness](../items.md#the-heart-of-darkness)** by slowly poisoning the Winter Worm at the bottom of Lake Zarovich. [Ludmilla](strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic) runs this with [Baba Lysaga](baba-lysaga.md)'s help, via upstream runoff from the Luna River. He is escalating the dose during the party's time in Vallaki, and plans to send [Rahadin](rahadin.md) down to kill it. His offer to the party is a task force "to remove the foul presence poisoning Vallaki," rewarded with a favor, safe exit, and a vault item under magical contract. See [Strahd's Machination: Poisoning the Winter Worm](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-machination-poisoning-the-winter-worm) and [Strahd's Offer to the Party, the Bones of Saint Andral & the Brides](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-offer-to-the-party-the-bones-of-saint-andral--the-brides). An ideation draft of his own Tarokka reading is in [Strahd's Own Tarokka Reading (ideation draft, not canon)](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-own-tarokka-reading-ideation-draft-not-canon).
