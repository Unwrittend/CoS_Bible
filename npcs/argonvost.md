### Argonvost

**Source characterization:** RAW;, reworked timeline

  

**Role:** Dragon-in-disguise; founder and guardian of the Amber Temple; later founder-by-circumstance of the Order of the Silver Dragon

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - A dragon disguised as a nobleman; settled near the Amber Temple specifically to guard it as "a repository of evil power" long before [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md) ever rose to power — not to fight Strahd, and without awareness the Fanes were sealed inside.
  - Ironic detail: by preventing anything from leaving the Amber Temple, his vigilance inadvertently made his order the Spring Fane's jailer too, without ever knowing she was there.
  - Initially treats Strahd as a non-issue as long as Strahd doesn't interfere with the Temple — a live-and-let-live posture that [Saint Markovia](saint-markovia.md) is the one who eventually breaks by convincing him appeasement doesn't guarantee safety.
  - Keeps the [Sun Sword](../items.md#the-sun-sword) inside the Amber Temple itself; Markovia must prove herself worthy before he arms her and lends his support.
  - Survives Strahd's second death; issues a call to arms afterward that recruits [Reysatra](reysatra.md) (the "Silver Age" — see [Cosmology](../lore/cosmology.md)).
  - Secretly maneuvered by [Madam Eva](madam-eva.md) into believing the idea to dissipate Amber Shards out of the Temple was his own, when her real goal was extracting the Spring Fane piece by piece.
  - Killed, along with the rest of the Silver Dragon Knights, when Strahd returns from his second death — his remains kept as a trophy in Castle Ravenloft rather than consecrated ground, which is why Strahd is able to target and destroy the order directly.
  - Optional, undecided: whether he and Katarina/Madam Eva had a personal history (courtly acquaintance at minimum, possibly an unconsummated romance predating her transformation).

  

**Current narrative status:** Historical figure, deceased for centuries by the party's present day. Not yet encountered; his Holt (per the party's card-reading tracker) is tied to the [Holy Symbol of Ravenkind](../items.md#the-holy-symbol--amulet-of-ravenkind)'s location. **[Session 9](../sessions/s09.md) update:** [Ireena](ireena-kolyana.md)'s in-fiction guidance narrowed this further — the Holt ("a house led by a dragon, died over a hundred years ago") is reachable via an offshoot of the Luna River.
