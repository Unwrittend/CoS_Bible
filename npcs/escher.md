### Escher Targolovich

**Source characterization:** [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md) Reloaded (NPC appendix), adapted. Reloaded's Escher is one of Strahd's consorts, not formally married to him and an inferior member of the court: publicly flamboyant and self-assured, privately guilt-ridden, self-loathing and desperate for Strahd's approval. **Not used in this campaign (Chris, 2026-11-17):** Reloaded's backstory crush on Doru, and Reloaded's morale note that he'd rather plead or flee than fight.

**Role:** Strahd's newest consort. **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** The brides' agent on the ground in Vallaki, executing the St. Andral's bones plot from [Henrik](henrik-van-der-voort.md)'s house.

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Desperately seeking approval. Everything he does in Vallaki is an attempt to get into the good graces of [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md) and of [Anastrasya](strahds-brides.md#anastrasya-karelova).
  - The fan guide's view (via [Ludmilla](strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic)) still frames how the court sees him: the newest, least-tested consort, held in open contempt by Ludmilla.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-24 ideation** (see [Strahd's Brides](strahds-brides.md)): he attaches himself to [Volenta](strahds-brides.md#volenta-popofsky), hoping to manipulate her.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-11-17 ideation:** in Vallaki, **Anastrasya orchestrates the bones plot and Escher is simply the executor on the ground.** He replaces Anastrasya at the coffin maker's house in the [Session 12 prep](../prep/s12-vallaki.md): he sets the traps there (vampire spawn and zombies) and moves the bones out. What Anastrasya herself is doing now is left open.
  - **GM-confirmed 2026-11-17 (not seen by the party):** **Escher**, not Anastrasya, was the one present at the Blue Water Inn to see [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md)'s attack (the party failed the Perception check). *(What changed: the [Session 12](../sessions/s12.md) prep had Anastrasya there.)*

**Appearance** (from Chris's reference art, 2026-11-17): long gold hair, pointed ears, red eyes, a black ribbon at the throat, a wineglass in hand. In his bloodlust form: hair wild, fangs bared, blood down his chin.

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play; his name hasn't been spoken at the table.

#### Stat block [IDEA — NONCANONICAL] (built in Foundry, 2026-11-17)

A two-form boss on the [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md) Reloaded bride template: each form has its own 82 HP, and the change happens at 0 HP. Form 1 is the duelist with bard magic; Form 2 is the bloodlust phase. Budget: about 5,200 XP across both forms, just under deadly for five level-5 PCs (party level assumed; not recorded in the bible). Radiant damage shuts off his Regeneration.

##### Form 1: Gallant of the Court

*Medium undead, neutral evil* · **Challenge 6 (2,300 XP)**

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +3, Cha +7  
**Skills** Acrobatics +7, Deception +7, Perception +3, Performance +10, Persuasion +7  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common

**Traits**

***Regeneration.*** Escher regains 10 hit points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If he takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn.

***Spider Climb.*** Escher can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

***Fast Grappler.*** Escher doesn't spend extra movement to move a creature he is grappling, if it is his size or smaller.

***Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** While in sunlight, Escher takes 20 radiant damage at the start of his turn, and he has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

***Rakish Audacity.*** Escher adds his Charisma modifier to initiative rolls (+8 total).

***Red Thirst.*** When Escher drops to 0 hit points, he transforms into Form 2 on the same initiative count. Excess damage doesn't carry over, and any conditions on him remain.

**Actions**

***Multiattack.*** Escher makes three Rapier attacks, or two Rapier attacks and one Vicious Mockery.

***Rapier.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

***Vicious Mockery.*** One creature within 60 ft. that can hear Escher must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or take 5 (2d4) psychic damage and have disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

***Dissonant Whispers (2/Day).*** One creature within 60 ft. that can hear Escher must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage and must immediately use its reaction, if available, to move as far as its speed allows away from Escher. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and doesn't have to move.

***Thunderwave (1/Day).*** Each creature in a 15-foot cube originating from Escher must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 9 (2d8) thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

***Ballroom Flourish (Recharge 5–6).*** Escher moves up to his speed and makes one Rapier attack against each creature he moves within 5 feet of. None of those creatures can make opportunity attacks against him during this move.

**Bonus Actions**

***Issue Challenge.*** Escher chooses one creature he can see within 30 ft. as his Partner; choosing a new Partner ends the previous challenge. The Partner must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than Escher. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. Escher has advantage on Rapier attacks against his Partner while no other hostile creature is within 5 ft. of him.

***Disarming Smile.*** One creature within 10 ft. of Escher must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 1 minute or until Escher loses concentration (as if concentrating on a spell). The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Reactions** (up to 3 per round, only 1 per turn)

***Parry.*** Escher adds 3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him.

***Riposte.*** When a creature within 5 ft. misses Escher with a melee attack, he makes one Rapier attack against it.

***Cutting Words (3/Day).*** When a creature within 60 ft. that Escher can see makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll, Escher subtracts 1d8 from the roll.

##### Form 2: The Bloodsick Duelist

*Medium undead, neutral evil* · **Challenge 7 (2,900 XP)**

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 82 (11d8 + 33)  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

Ability scores, saving throws, skills, resistances, senses and languages are the same as Form 1.

**Traits**

***Regeneration, Spider Climb, Fast Grappler, Sunlight Hypersensitivity.*** As Form 1.

***Blood Frenzy.*** Escher has advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its hit points.

***Scent of Blood.*** Escher knows the location of every creature within 60 ft. that doesn't have all its hit points, and of any hidden creature with a heartbeat within 30 ft.

**Actions**

***Multiattack.*** Escher makes two Claws attacks and one Bite attack.

***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage. Instead of dealing damage, Escher can grapple the target (escape DC 14).

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature that is grappled by Escher, incapacitated, or restrained, or that both of Escher's Claws attacks hit this turn. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and Escher regains hit points equal to that amount. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

***Pounce (Recharge 5–6).*** Escher moves up to 30 ft. in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks, then makes one Claws attack. On a hit, the target is also grappled (escape DC 14), and Escher can make one Bite attack against it as part of this action.

**Bonus Actions**

***Hungry Advance.*** Escher moves up to half his speed toward a creature that doesn't have all its hit points, without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Reactions** (up to 3 per round, only 1 per turn)

***Rake.*** When a creature within 5 ft. misses Escher with a melee attack, he makes one Claws attack against it.

***Aggress.*** After Escher takes damage from an attack or spell, he moves up to half his speed toward the attacker.
