### Baba Lysaga

**Source characterization:** Homebrew reworking of the published-module NPC, tied into the Fanes mythology

  

**Role:** Former priestess of the Spring Fane; [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md)'s self-styled "true mother"; exiled to Berez

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - A former priestess (not necessarily high priestess) of the Spring Fane, motivated by love for Strahd and a desire to absorb the Spring Fane's memory-collecting power to become, in her own mind, Strahd's "true mother."
  - Captured the Spring Fane in amber — this is floated (not confirmed) as the origin/etymology of "Amber Temple," though this needs reconciling with the Temple's established deeper history as an ancient nexus predating her.
  - Exiled by Strahd (via [Rahadin](rahadin.md)) to the ruins of Berez — not yet ruins at the time of her exile — after he grew unable to tolerate her instability.
  - Later performs a ritual sacrifice using Berez itself as fuel to resurrect Strahd after his second ([Markovia](saint-markovia.md)-era) death, drawing on/further depleting the Spring Fane's power she already holds captive — offered as the explanation for both her present comparative weakness and Berez's eventual ruin.
  - Occupies a dual faction position: tied to Strahd directly, with acknowledged (unresolved) connections to the Dark Powers as well.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-19:** helps [Ludmilla](strahds-brides.md#ludmilla-vilisevic) craft the poison slowly killing the Winter Worm ([Strahd's Machination: Poisoning the Winter Worm](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-machination-poisoning-the-winter-worm)). Her **creeping hut** is floated as an automaton imitation of the Spring Fane's primordial spider ([The Primordial Beasts](../archive/s10-prep.md#the-primordial-beasts)). **Unresolved:** whether she has covens/pupils/agents (Chris's view) or works alone (Michelle's prior assumption). Folk tales may cast her as a healer whose cures come at a great price ([Vallaki's Factions (current working model)](../archive/s10-prep.md#vallakis-factions-current-working-model)).

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play; tied to the ruins of Berez, an unvisited location.
