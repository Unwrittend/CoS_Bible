### Petrina Velikanova

**Source characterization:** RAW;

  

**Role:** Casimir Velikov's dead sister; a banshee trapped in Castle Ravenloft's catacombs; a candidate "lost soul" for the Tome of Strahd questline

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - Sends dreams to her brother Casimir, who wants to resurrect her.
  - Tentatively identified as the "lost soul" from the Nine-of-Glyphs card clue tied to the Tome of Strahd (see Section 11).

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play; located in Castle Ravenloft's catacombs.
