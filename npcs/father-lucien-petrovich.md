### Father Lucien Petrovich

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** Source: 2026-09-19 ideation (Section 15.7B).

**Source characterization:** RAW. **Spelling confirmed 2026-09-24: Lucien** (earlier entries spelled it "Lucian"; all references updated). The Session 10 Prep doc's gates scene shows him wearing the Morninglord's crest.

**Role:** Priest of St. Andral's Church, Vallaki

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Works outside the gates among the Barovian refugees with his altar boy **Yeska**, trying to heal the sick with holy power.
  - Doesn't know what the "withering sickness" is, but has noticed many Barovians wasting away and dying in droves. He is the NPC who talks about the sickness.
  - Would likely know Father Donavich.
  - Custodian of St. Andral's bones, which keep Vallaki's ground consecrated (and, per ideation, may be what protects its wells — Section 15.5).

  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation (Section 16):** Faction note: **Yeska** shares Lucien's motives (not a separate faction). The church's gravedigger **Milivoj** is the one who steals St. Andral's bones and hands them to the coffin maker **Henrik van der Voort** (per RAW). Who Milivoj is working for — and how this fits Anastrasya's bones heist (15.5) — is unreconciled (Section 14).

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play.
