### Father Lucien Petrovich

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** Source: 2026-09-19 ideation ([Planned Encounters for the Next Session (B)](../archive/s10-prep.md#planned-encounters-for-the-next-session)).

**Source characterization:** RAW. **Spelling confirmed 2026-09-24: Lucien** (earlier entries spelled it "Lucian"; all references updated). The [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) Prep doc's gates scene shows him wearing the Morninglord's crest.

**Role:** Priest of St. Andral's Church, Vallaki

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Works outside the gates among the Barovian refugees with his altar boy **[Yeska](yeska.md)**, trying to heal the sick with holy power.
  - Doesn't know what the "withering sickness" is, but has noticed many Barovians wasting away and dying in droves. He is the NPC who talks about the sickness.
  - Would likely know Father Donavich.
  - Custodian of St. Andral's bones, which keep Vallaki's ground consecrated (and, per ideation, may be what protects its wells — [Strahd's Offer to the Party, the Bones of Saint Andral & the Brides](../archive/s10-prep.md#strahds-offer-to-the-party-the-bones-of-saint-andral--the-brides)).

  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation ([Section 16](../prep/s11-vallaki.md)):** Faction note: **Yeska** shares Lucien's motives (not a separate faction). The church's gravedigger **[Milivoj](milivoj.md)** is the one who steals St. Andral's bones and hands them to the coffin maker **[Henrik van der Voort](henrik-van-der-voort.md)** (per RAW). Who Milivoj is working for — and how this fits [Anastrasya](strahds-brides.md#anastrasya-karelova)'s bones heist (15.5) — is unreconciled ([Section 14](../open-questions.md)).

**Current narrative status:** **[Session 11.5](../sessions/s11-5.md) (canonical):** first seen, not yet spoken to. On the full-moon night he led a packed congregation at St. Andral's in prayer from the lectern, "with authority but kindness," wearing the Morninglord's sun symbol, and is to pray all night. [Reyna](../pcs/reyna.md) is to speak with him in the morning.

  

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-10-27 ideation ([Session 12 prep](../prep/s12-vallaki.md)):** Lucien **supports [Vargas](baron-vargas-volokovich.md)**. He is disappointed in him but thinks him well-meaning: as Lucien understands it, Vargas walks a tightrope where any risk could harm his people, so he is single-mindedly devoted to Vallakians, at the Barovians' expense. Lucien keeps helping the refugees in small ways as a spiritual leader, and Vargas gives him that leeway because he believes festivals alone can't protect Vallaki without the church. They need each other politically: the church is a political conduit, and Lucien's soft power (the ear of the people) depends on not opposing Vargas.
  - **On [Stella](stella-watcher.md):** if the party raises the diary, he dismisses it as propaganda from Madame Watcher. Stella is happily married to [Victor](victor-volokovich.md); [Lady Watcher](lady-fiona-watcher.md) disliked the match, but it was needed to keep the family in power. He has visited Stella himself: she suffers an illness that his tinctures and the Morninglord's power haven't cured, "insidious ailments of the devil."
  - **On the sickness:** he calls the parasites weapons of [Strahd](strahd-von-zarovich.md), and also blames **Vistani curses**: Strahd and "his sycophantic followers" camp outside the walls begging to be let in, spreading infection through the water. The Vistani don't worship the Morninglord. The GMs want him to be both: genuinely on the party's side and openly prejudiced. [Emeric](emeric.md) and [Magda](magda.md) recognize and trust him, which gives the party a reason to see him as an ally anyway. He has probably never met a dusk elf, so [Kasimir](kasimir-velikov.md) doesn't trigger it the same way; if wanted, it can surface as remarks about elven ears.
  - He won't suspect the party of the bones theft; they were in his sight most of the night. Expected to explain the chains and locks: Strahd steals Vallaki's children (perhaps lullabies carry them away). He names Strahd as the culprit, not "Strahd's minions."
