### Father Lucien Petrovich

**[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]** Source: 2026-09-19 ideation ([Section 15](../archive/s10-prep.md).7B).

**Source characterization:** RAW. **Spelling confirmed 2026-09-24: Lucien** (earlier entries spelled it "Lucian"; all references updated). The [Session 10](../sessions/s10.md) Prep doc's gates scene shows him wearing the Morninglord's crest.

**Role:** Priest of St. Andral's Church, Vallaki

**Key characterization principles:**

  - Works outside the gates among the Barovian refugees with his altar boy **Yeska**, trying to heal the sick with holy power.
  - Doesn't know what the "withering sickness" is, but has noticed many Barovians wasting away and dying in droves. He is the NPC who talks about the sickness.
  - Would likely know Father Donavich.
  - Custodian of St. Andral's bones, which keep Vallaki's ground consecrated (and, per ideation, may be what protects its wells — [Section 15.5](../archive/s10-prep.md#155--strahds-offer-to-the-party-the-bones-of-saint-andral--the-brides)).

  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation ([Section 16](../prep/s11-vallaki.md)):** Faction note: **Yeska** shares Lucien's motives (not a separate faction). The church's gravedigger **Milivoj** is the one who steals St. Andral's bones and hands them to the coffin maker **[Henrik van der Voort](henrik-van-der-voort.md)** (per RAW). Who Milivoj is working for — and how this fits [Anastrasya](strahds-brides.md#anastrasya-karelova)'s bones heist (15.5) — is unreconciled ([Section 14](../open-questions.md)).

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play.
