### Gertruda Stiegler

**Source characterization:** Dragnacarta's reimagining (primary) + Bella Swann / *Twilight* (voice reference for specific dialogue and internal monologue work)

  

**Personal Homebrew:** Gertruda is the reincarnation of Jacob's Gertruda. Gertruda lived one full life in Barovia trying to escape, but her soul is trapped in Barovia. She has reincarnated as Gertruda Stiegler.

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - She engages in **self-aware introspection** — she notices her own reactions and questions them
  - She does **not** present as entirely victim or entirely complicit; she is a person making choices in difficult conditions
  - Her voice should feel grounded and modern-adjacent in interiority (per the Bella reference) — relatable selfhood inside an extraordinary situation

  

**Voice notes for dialogue/letters:**

  

  - First-person register is direct and observational, not florid
  - She notices specific sensory details — what things smell like, what silences feel like
  - Romantic tension (if applicable) is described from the inside — her own recognition of being pulled, not the pull itself

**Planned development \[IDEA — NONCANONICAL\]:**

  

  - Jacob's connection to Gertruda is being put on hold as her primary motivating thread — she needs to work as a character independent of him.
  - Begins taking on the role of an early apprentice to Von Richten — not loyalty, but practical survival strategy. Her ultimate goal remains escaping Barovia entirely, since as a reincarnated soul she believes she doesn't belong there.
  - Why Von Richten keeps her around: some mix of not-yet-proven usefulness, and that she cooks for him (he's been living on rations/swill).
  - Writes her diary/notes in Common, not Giant (the only other language readily available) — she's smart enough not to leave notes Von Richten himself could read. Aware she's replaceable to him and plays the relationship carefully.
  - Characterization: not a combatant — assassin/bard-adjacent build, storyteller personality, street-smart, acts older than her apparent age (14) due to past-life memories. May perform naivety around Von Richten as a survival tactic while actually being quite calculating.
  - *A bit floated and set aside as flavor, not canon: Gertruda secretly writing romance novels for the Vallaki book trade, with Von Richten as her unwitting muse.*
