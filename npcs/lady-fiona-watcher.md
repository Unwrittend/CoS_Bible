### Lady Fiona Watcher

**Source characterization:** RAW + Homebrew expanded

  

**Role:** Matriarch of the pro-Strahd Watcher family in Vallaki

  

**Key characterization principles:**

  

  - Per raw material: secretly runs a devil-worship cult using a hidden imp to fake a summoning ritual for her followers; sleeps with her own dead husband's corpse (preserved via Gentle Repose); married her daughter Stella to Victor Volokovich as a political maneuver.
  - Redeeming trait worked out for this campaign: fierce, politically savvy commitment to keeping her old, declining noble house alive, whatever the personal or moral cost — explicitly likened to a Game of Thrones "Tyrell" archetype. Whether she genuinely believes Strahd is Barovia's rightful ruler is left ambiguous; what's certain is she believes her house dies with this generation (her only daughter is mad, her sons Nikolai II and Carl are apathetic and oblivious to both her cult and their father's corpse), and aligning with Strahd is, in her calculation, the only way to save it.
  - Stella's marriage to Victor broke Stella's sanity after his cruelty; Fiona now keeps her locked away. Stella's madness is floated as an alternate "lost soul" lead into the Tome of Strahd mystery (see Section 11), though this branch ties less cleanly into the wider Vallaki plot than the Casimir/Petrina branch.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-15 ideation — Watcher House goal:** re-establish the House's power and standing, either by curing Stella's madness or by positioning the House to survive Strahd's new era (currying favor, or taking ownership of Vallaki). The party curing Stella would complete her goal. Possible means, not settled: seeking help from one of Strahd's brides (most likely Ludmilla, a known mage) or from Baba Lysaga via a coven intermediary (folk tales cast Lysaga as a healer whose cures come at great price). Her imp familiar comes from self-taught occultism, possibly coven-learned. See Section 15.6.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] Family dynamics:** the sons lack her ambition and temper, and drink and party to escape the loss of their sister. The family was once close; grief broke it, and the sons think their mother has gone a little mad.
  - **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL] 2026-09-27 / 10-08 ideation (Section 16):** **The Watcher rebellion.** Fiona will incite an uprising against Vargas **with or without the players**. It comes to a head at the Festival of the Blazing Sun (day 3), or sooner if she wins the party's support. It's averted only if she's dissuaded or becomes Burgomistress first. Chris likes the idea of Fiona hero-worshipping one of the party's clerics as a prophesied savior (adapted from MandyMod; which cleric is undecided). She is also considering **assassinating Izek** and has Ernst mapping his patrols.
  - **Naming RESOLVED (confirmed 2026-09-24):** the dead husband is **Nikolai** and the son is **Nikolai II**, named after his father. *(What changed: an earlier REVISION flag treated the 2026-09-15 ideation's dead husband "Nikolai" as conflicting with this entry's son "Nikolai"; both are correct.)* The husband's death — of sickness three years ago, per the 09-15 ideation (Chris had pictured ~1 year) — remains **[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]**.

  

**Current narrative status:** Not yet encountered in play.
