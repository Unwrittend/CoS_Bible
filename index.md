# Curse of Strahd — Campaign Bible

GM reference for Chris and Michelle's *Curse of Strahd* (Dragnacarta reimagining) campaign, split into one small file per character, session and topic.

## Start here

1. **[Current state](current-state.md)**: where the party is and what's open, in one page.
2. **[Entity index](entity-index.md)**: every PC, NPC, session and lore file, with aliases and canon status.
3. **[Conventions](conventions.md)**: tone guide, plus the maintenance and canonicity rules (`[IDEA — NONCANONICAL]`, `REVISION`).
